## Founding history (verbatim from Messari "Bittensor Profile" section)

> Bittensor was founded by Jacob Robert Steeves and Ala Shaabana to build a
> decentralized market for the production of machine intelligence. According to
> third-party CBInsights, Bittensor was founded in 2019, though Steeves
> LinkedIn states he has worked on Bittensor since March 2016.

> In January 2021, Opentensor Foundation launched the first iteration of
> Subtensor called "Kusanagi." In May 2021, Opentensor Foundation halted
> Kusanagi "to address a few early issues with consensus." In November 2021,
> Kusanagi was forked and launched under the name "Nakamoto." Notably, 546,113
> TAO (~2.61% of the total token supply) previously mined on Kusanagi were
> migrated to Nakamoto.

> The testnet version of "Finney" launched on Jan. 10, 2023... On March 20,
> 2023, Nakamoto was forked to Finney mainnet "to address a few performance
> issues in the network's kernel code," and remains as the live version of
> Subtensor.

## Security events (per Messari Q&A)

- **2024 PyPi Package Manager breach**: A compromised package led to private-key
  exposure; **32,000+ TAO tokens stolen**
- Following the breach, Bittensor entered "safe mode" — halted transactions and
  shielded validators with firewalls
- **February 2025**: **$25M Nexus Mutual insurance cover** against smart
  contract hacks
