# io.net — Incentive Dynamic Engine (IDE) Launch Announcement

- **URL:** https://io.net/blog/incentive-dynamic-engine
- **Published:** December 11, 2025
- **Fetched:** 2026-05-26
- **Reliability:** Company-generated

## Core claim

> Decentralized infrastructure isn't built on hype and empty promises. It's
> built on sustainable economics that actually delivers value.

## IDE mechanism (verbatim)

> The IDE is a first-of-its-kind fully adaptive, real-time economic controller
> for decentralized infrastructure. Unlike fixed emissions models, it adjusts
> token supply dynamically based on actual GPU usage through a sustainability
> ratio.

> When network revenue exceeds supplier payout obligations, tokens are burned
> or stored to create deflationary pressure. When revenue falls short, the
> system temporarily expands supply to maintain stable supplier income. This
> self-regulating mechanism keeps the network resilient regardless of market
> conditions.

## Stated outcomes

- Suppliers receive **predictable USD-denominated payouts**, insulated from
  token volatility
- Token holders benefit from deflationary mechanics: **at least 50% of net
  revenue permanently burned**
- Potential cumulative reduction: **up to 150 million $IO** from circulating
  supply

## Acknowledgment of prior model failures (verbatim)

> io.net experienced these challenges firsthand. Our initial tokenomics system
> got us off the ground but created misalignment between token supply and
> actual network value. That's when it became clear that we needed a
> fundamentally different tokenomic system.

## Implementation timeline

- Litepaper released: December 11, 2025
- Community feedback: through February 27, 2026
- Final version: March 31, 2026
- Implementation: Q2 2026 (around io.net's second anniversary)

## Stated benefits beyond crypto

> The IDE establishes a framework for sustainable, utility-driven networks that
> can democratize access to compute resources and reduce infrastructure costs
> across the AI development landscape.

## Notes for analyst

- This is **the most candid admission of failed tokenomics by any AI-DePIN
  project to date** in the sources reviewed. Direct acknowledgment that the
  pre-IDE token model "created misalignment between token supply and actual
  network value."
- The 97% drawdown in $IO price from ATH ($6.44 → $0.18) maps to this
  acknowledged misalignment.
- The IDE is a credible structural response, but **un-implemented as of late
  May 2026**. Investment thesis depends on:
  1. Q2 2026 implementation arriving on schedule
  2. Actual revenue generation supporting the burn mechanism (50% of net rev)
  3. GPU supplier retention through the transition
- Compare to Bittensor's Taoflow (already implemented November 2025), Gonka's
  Bitcoin-style halving (already implemented December 2025). io.net is
  meaningfully **late** to the demand-driven tokenomics redesign cycle.
