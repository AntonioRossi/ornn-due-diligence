- **97% drawdown from ATH** — among the most severe drawdowns in the AI-DePIN

- Vol/Mcap of 157% is extremely high — daily volume exceeds market cap,
  consistent with **high-turnover speculative trading rather than utility
  demand**.
- 84,870 holders is meaningful — much larger than Gonka (845) and significantly
  smaller than Bittensor (large CEX-based holder base).
- The Solana SPL contract address is the **definitive on-chain reference** for
  IO; this is a pure Solana token, not a multi-chain or wrapped asset.
- CMC text lists "Tory Green" as current CEO; the io.net 2025 year-in-review
  references **Gaurav Sharma** as the CEO who gave the "Decentralize or Die"
  keynote in Singapore. This **CEO discrepancy** is unresolved and material
  for diligence.
