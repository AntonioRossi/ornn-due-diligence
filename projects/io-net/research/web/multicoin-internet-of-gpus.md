## Deal facts

- **Multicoin led the Seed round and participated in the Series A**
- **Series A: $30M total** from Multicoin, Hack VC, 6th Man Ventures, Modular
  Capital, plus a "syndicate of deeply connected angel investors"
- First meeting between Sengupta and Shadid: **Austin Solana Hacker House,
  April 2023**
- **57,000 compute hours** facilitated by io.net at March 2024 publication
- **BC8.ai** (fine-tuned variant of Stable Diffusion) was trained on io.net
- Inferences recorded on-chain for provenance; example payment to "a cluster
  of 6 RTX 4090s"

## Thesis (verbatim, condensed)

> Demand for AI compute is growing at a breakneck pace; demand is insatiable.
> Datacenter revenues for AI workloads topped $100 billion in 2023, but the
> demand for AI outstrips chip supply in even the most conservative of cases.

> io.net is a classic instantiation of the DePIN thesis: using token
> incentives to structurally lower the cost of acquiring and retaining
> supply-side resources, and ultimately reducing costs for end-consumers.

> Today, the network is powered by thousands of GPUs from datacenters,
> mining farms, and consumer-grade devices.

## Technical positioning

- Uses **Ray** (Anyscale, open source), **Ludwig**, **Kubernetes**, and
  "a variety of other open-source, distributed computing frameworks."
- Hardware sources at launch: data centers, mining farms, consumer-grade
  devices (specifically RTX 4090s and similar).
- Integration with Render Network consumer nodes mentioned.

## Cost-structure framing

> They achieve these cost savings by outsourcing GPU coordination and
> overhead to the protocol. Cloud providers, on the other hand, markup
> infrastructure costs because they have employee expenses, hardware
> maintenance, and datacenter overhead. The opportunity cost for consumer
> card clusters and mining farms is substantially lower than what
> Hyperscalers are willing to accept, thus a structural arbitrage exists that
> dynamically prices resources on io.net lower than the ever-increasing cloud
> rate.

## Sengupta's framing of Shadid (verbatim)

> I first met io.net's founder, Ahmad Shadid, at the Austin Solana Hacker
> House in April 2023, and was immediately drawn to his singular focus on
> democratizing access to compute resources for ML workloads.

> In the time since, the io.net team has executed against the thesis at light
> speed.

## Notes for analyst

- **This is the lead investor's primary public thesis document, written
  three months before Shadid's June 2024 resignation.** It does not anticipate
  the subsequent founder-departure or misrepresentation allegations.
- Multicoin's continued portfolio position post-Shadid is **load-bearing
  for the institutional thesis** — if Multicoin remained invested through
  the founder transition, the position is implicitly an endorsement of
  current management. If Multicoin exited or wrote down, that is a negative
  signal. Public disclosure of Multicoin's current TAO holdings is not
  available.
- The "57,000 compute hours" figure (March 2024) is useful as an early
  baseline. The 2025 year-in-review reported 552,000 GPU hours for Wondera
  alone — orders of magnitude more, suggesting genuine usage growth.
- The "structural arbitrage" cost framing — consumer/mining farm opportunity
  cost lower than hyperscaler-rates — is the core unit-economics thesis. The
  IDE Q2 2026 implementation is designed to make this sustainable through
  market cycles.
