## Notes for analyst

- This is the **single most consequential finding for the io.net IC memo**.
  The founder-risk signal is structurally identical to founder-fraud patterns
  documented in prior crypto cycle blowups (with no allegation of intentional
  fraud at this stage — only "misrepresentation" allegations).
- The fact that **IO.net paid a six-figure severance specifically to distance
  itself from Shadid** is itself a signal that the company's own counsel
  recognized founder-risk exposure to the protocol post-token-launch.
- IO.net's own subsequent communications (CEO transition, IDE tokenomics
  redesign, December 2025 acknowledgment that "our initial tokenomics system
  … created misalignment between token supply and actual network value") are
  consistent with active distancing from the Shadid-era protocol design.
- For the IC memo, this finding does not preclude investment — IO.net under
  Tory Green / Gaurav Sharma may be substantively different from Shadid-era
  IO.net — but it **materially raises the bar** for what current management
  must demonstrate.
