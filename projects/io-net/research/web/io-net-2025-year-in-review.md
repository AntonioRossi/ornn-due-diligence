## Major December 2025 release: Incentive Dynamic Engine (IDE)

- Released December 11, 2025
- **Demand-driven tokenomics** replacing fixed inflation
- Dual-vault mechanism stabilizes GPU provider payouts in USD terms
- **At least 50% of remaining net revenue burned** (targeting 150M+ $IO supply
  reduction)
- When revenue > obligations: tokens absorbed from circulation
- When revenue < obligations: temporary supply expansion to maintain stable
  supplier returns
- Community feedback through **February 27**; final version **March 31**;
  implementation **Q2 2026**

## Notes for analyst

- **Network size discrepancy**: homepage and search blurbs claim "30,000+ GPUs"
  but this year-in-review documents "2,752 verified GPUs." The gap is material
  for any market-size or revenue triangulation. Likely the 30k+ number includes
  unverified, occasionally-online, or pending-onboarding GPUs.
- **CEO Gaurav Sharma is the operational leader** as of this 2025 year-in-
  review. CMC older copy refers to **Tory Green** as CEO. Either there's been
  a transition or one source is outdated.
- The customer case studies are the **strongest evidence of real external
  consumption** for any AI-DePIN token reviewed in this firm's portfolio
  (Bittensor's Corcel + Macrocosmos are stronger but fewer).
- The IDE is **the most aggressive demand-driven tokenomic redesign** announced
  in the AI-DePIN sector — it explicitly addresses the death-spiral pattern
  that Bittensor's Taoflow also aimed to fix, but via burn instead of EMA flow
  rebalancing.
