# Management diligence request list — IO Research / io.net (IO)

Tailored to **io.net / IO Research** as of **May 26, 2026** and based on public information only. The public record — a Solana-native decentralized GPU cloud running since April 2024, $30M Series A from a tier-1 syndicate (Hack VC, Multicoin, Solana Labs, Aptos Labs, 6th Man Ventures, Modular Capital, Animoca Brands, OKX), 800M-supply IO token listed on Coinbase and Binance, named customer references with disclosed AWS-comparison savings (Leonardo.Ai, Wondera, Frodobots/UC Berkeley RAIL, Vistara Labs, KayOS), and the structurally sophisticated Incentive Dynamic Engine (IDE) tokenomic redesign — is sufficient to justify deeper diligence. **However, the founder-history overhang and the timing of the Q2 2026 IDE implementation justify pausing capital deployment until the gating issues below are resolved**: **(1) the Ahmad Shadid resignation, six-figure severance, and the "reported inflated GPU supply claims" allegation; (2) the IDE implementation timing and post-implementation observability; (3) the network-scale claim integrity (30,000+ vs 2,752 verified GPUs); (4) the missing legal entity disclosure for IO Research; (5) the absence of comprehensive smart-contract security audits; and (6) the current-CEO identity discrepancy (Tory Green per CoinMarketCap vs Gaurav Sharma per the company's own 2025 Year in Review).**

## Management diligence request list

**Instruction to IO Research:** please provide the following in a dataroom, with one folder owner per section, latest revision date on each file, and a short note indicating whether the file is final, draft, or board-only. Where a document does not exist, please say so explicitly rather than substituting a marketing-page equivalent. For items requiring on-chain data, read-only Solana RPC access or a static export at a specified block height is acceptable in lieu of a written report.

**Diligence sequencing:** populate all **P0 / gating** items first. No IO token purchase, treasury allocation, OTC purchase, or strategic-partnership investment in IO Research or any affiliated entity should be considered until the P0 package is substantially complete, management interviews are held, and (critically) the Q2 2026 IDE implementation is observable through at least one full post-implementation quarter.

### A. Corporate, legal, and regulatory (P0 / gating)

1. **IO Research legal entity disclosure**

   * Confirm the legal form, jurisdiction of incorporation, registered office, and date of incorporation of IO Research (or the entity that owns the io.net protocol, the IO token-issuance program, and the io.net domain).
   * Provide the board of directors / managing committee with named members, biographies, related-party disclosures, and tenure.
   * Provide Articles of Association / equivalent constitutional documents.
   * Provide audited (or reviewed if audit not yet performed) financial statements for the most recent two fiscal years, with notes on IO treasury holdings, fiat treasury, payroll, and any related-party transactions.
   * Confirm whether IO Research is the same entity that signed the Series A subscription agreements, owns the io.net IP, and controls the IO token mint authority on Solana.

2. **IO token classification under US securities law**

   * Provide the outside-counsel opinion(s) addressing IO's classification under the Securities Act of 1933 and the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, with explicit treatment of each of the following separately:
     - (a) IO emissions to GPU suppliers (currently inflationary; post-IDE, USD-stable USD-equivalent payouts)
     - (b) IO emissions to delegators / stakers (if any)
     - (c) IO purchased on Coinbase, Binance, and other tier-1 venues by US persons
     - (d) IO token-launch distribution and initial allocations
     - (e) The IDE's burn mechanism and any reflexive deflationary effects on IO holders
   * Confirm whether IO Research has received any SEC, CFTC, FinCEN, OFAC, or state-AG inquiries, subpoenas, no-action requests, or Wells notices.
   * Provide the listing-agreement documentation and any conditions imposed by Coinbase, Binance, OKX, Kraken, and other major exchanges.

3. **Non-US regulatory analysis**

   * Outside-counsel memos for: EU (MiCA), UK (FCA), Singapore (MAS), ADGM (FSRA), DIFC, Hong Kong (SFC), Switzerland (FINMA), Canada (CSA), Japan (FSA), Australia (ASIC).
   * Specifically address: MiCA classification of IO; FCA "qualifying cryptoasset" framework; any geo-blocking applied for US persons.

4. **Founder-history and severance documentation**

   * Provide the **executed Ahmad Shadid separation agreement** including the severance amount, vesting/clawback provisions, non-disclosure and non-disparagement terms, retained equity or governance rights, and any IO-token-related provisions.
   * Confirm Shadid's current IO token holdings (whether held in his own wallets, in a multisig, or held in escrow under the separation agreement).
   * Confirm whether Shadid retains any role, advisory position, board seat, equity, or contractual right with respect to IO Research or any affiliated entity.
   * Provide IO Research's official written position on the **"reported inflated GPU supply claims" allegation** documented in the Investigations.org May 2026 report, including any internal investigation findings.
   * Confirm whether the Investigations.org report has been formally challenged, corrected, or supplemented.

5. **Litigation, enforcement, and regulatory inquiry history**

   * For IO Research, named officers, and the io.net Network: all current and prior litigation, arbitration, enforcement actions, regulatory inquiries (formal or informal), subpoenas, government investigations, and threatened or actual cease-and-desist letters.
   * Specifically address any matters arising from Shadid's tenure that were not concluded prior to his departure.

### C. Technology, security, and operations (P0 / gating)

9. **Comprehensive smart-contract code audit package**

   * Provide all completed third-party audits (Trail of Bits, Halborn, Quantstamp, OtterSec, NCC Group, OpenZeppelin, etc.) for:
     - Solana program(s) implementing the IO token mint, staking, and reward distribution
     - The IDE vault contracts (Y₁ Reward Vault and Y₂ Fee Vault)
     - Any bridge contracts (if IO is bridged to other chains)
     - Off-chain coordination logic with on-chain reach (Proof-of-Compute oracle, etc.)
   * For each audit: scope, methodology, severity-tagged findings, resolution status, acknowledged-but-unresolved findings.
   * Confirm the live Bug Bounty program (Immunefi, HackenProof, or equivalent), with paid-bounties history and current scope.

10. **Network-scale claim integrity**

    * Define precisely what "GPU" means in (a) the homepage "30,000+ GPUs" claim and (b) the 2025 Year in Review "2,752 verified GPUs" figure. Reconcile the order-of-magnitude gap.
    * Provide the **verification methodology**: how is a GPU "verified"? What is the SLA threshold (uptime %, work submitted, staking deposit)?
    * Provide the on-chain dataset showing live, active, staked GPU count over the last 12 months, with breakdown by hardware class (H100, H200, A100, RTX 4090, etc.).
    * Provide the geographic distribution methodology (IP-geolocation? Self-attestation? Country-coded validator metadata?) for the "138+ countries" claim.
    * Provide the **trend data** showing whether the verified-GPU count has grown, plateaued, or contracted under post-Shadid management.

11. **Customer case-study substantiation**

    * For each of **Leonardo.Ai, Wondera, Frodobots × UC Berkeley RAIL Lab, Vistara Labs, KayOS**, provide:
      - The signed reference contact with permission for follow-up
      - Monthly inference-token volume or GPU-hour consumption

      - Contract term and any prepayment / commitment structure
      - Specific subnet / model mix used
    * Confirm whether any of these customers received subsidies, IO-token incentives, or revenue-share arrangements as part of their public-attributed savings figures.
    * Provide a **post-Shadid customer-cohort breakdown**: which customers were onboarded under Shadid's tenure (≤June 2024) vs under Green/Sharma's tenure (>June 2024).

12. **IDE Q2 2026 implementation status and contingency**

    * Provide the current implementation timeline (week-by-week if possible) for the IDE transition.
    * Engineering changelog and testnet results for the IDE Solana program implementation.
    * Stress-test results beyond the CryptoEcon Lab (CEL) 55% demand-drop / 50% token-price-crash scenarios — particularly: Solana chain outage scenarios, supplier mass-exit scenarios, and the documented "vault drift" risk.
    * Contingency plan if Q2 2026 implementation slips past Q3.
    * Named smart-contract audit firm engaged for the IDE deployment.
