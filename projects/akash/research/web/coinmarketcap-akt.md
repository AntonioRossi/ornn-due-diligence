## Notes for analyst

- **89% drawdown from ATH** — comparable to TAO (−63%) and worse than RNDR
  but better than IO (−97%) and GNK (−90%).
- **75.7% of max supply circulating** — meaningfully closer to max than IO
  (41.69%), TAO (52%), GNK (likely <30%). **Less remaining dilution risk.**
- CMC rank #127 places AKT in the **top 150 by market cap** — substantially
  higher than IO (#353), Gonka (#7957), comparable to many AI tokens.
- The "Cosmos Ecosystem" tag is **outdated as of October 2025** — Akash is
  migrating to Solana (or another chain). CMC has not updated the tagging.
- 0.26% Liq/Mcap is **structurally low** — institutional positions of
  meaningful size would move the price materially. This is a function of
  Akash's older tokenomics (large circulating float, deep retail dispersal,
  but no concentrated liquid pools).
- The ATH of $8.08 was set during the April 2021 alt cycle, before AI / DePIN
  narrative emerged. Akash has not retested ATH during the AI cycle. This is
  bullish framing (room to run if AI narrative re-engages) or bearish framing
  (failed to catch the prior cycle's momentum).
