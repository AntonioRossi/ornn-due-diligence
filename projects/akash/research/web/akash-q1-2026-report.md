## BME (Burn-Mint Equilibrium) — full deployment

> BME is the most significant change to AKT tokenomics since the network
> launched.

- **Proposal 318** passed and BME activated **March 23, 2026**
- Replaces prior AEP-23 (USDC-payment-only) model that was causing AKT to
  become irrelevant
- Mechanism: payment auto-buys AKT on the open market → burns it → mints ACT
  (non-transferable USD-pegged stablecoin) → settles transaction → provider
  receives ACT → provider can mint back to AKT
- Result: buyers get USD price stability; providers get USD-stable settlement;
  every compute transaction creates AKT demand
- Testnet validation: 10+ test categories, 250 participants, $10K incentives
- "Every workload on the network [is] a deflationary event for the first time
  in Akash's history"

## Homenode (consumer-GPU supply)

> Akash Homenode Beta opened sign-ups in Q1, introducing a new supply-side
> category that did not exist on Akash before: consumer and prosumer GPU
> compute contributed directly by individual hardware owners.

- Supported initial hardware: RTX 4090, RTX 5090, Quadro RTX 6000 Ada
- Purpose-built for AI inference (the high-volume, low-duration agent workload
  type)
- **Mesh-resilience framing**: "If a data center in the Middle East takes a
  strike, nodes in Seoul and Frankfurt keep running"
- Live access at homenode.akash.network

## Notes for analyst

- Q1 2026 was a **structural inflection** — BME live + Homenode + AkashML on
  OpenRouter + hackathon wins. This is the most concentrated set of positive
  catalysts among any AI-DePIN project reviewed.
- $5M total compute spend ATH is **meaningful but small** relative to
  Bittensor TAO's market cap dynamics. Compute spend on Akash translates
  through BME to AKT burn, but the absolute dollar volume is still tiny vs
  AWS/Azure markets.
- Homenode is a clear bet on consumer-edge supply expansion. If even 1% of
  the global RTX 4090 base joins, supply scales by orders of magnitude.
- The Cloudflare-outpacing claim on OpenRouter is striking but needs
  verification on the methodology (single-model? Or all models combined?).
