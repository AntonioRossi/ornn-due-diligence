# Kaspa Ecosystem Foundation (KEF)

Source: https://www.kaspafoundation.org/
Scraped: 2026-05-27

## Stated mission (verbatim)

> "Kaspa Ecosystem Foundation (KEF) is committed to Kaspa's long-term success, working on all fronts to solidify its place as a leader in blockchain technology. By spearheading cutting-edge research and development of the Kaspa mainnet, catalyzing innovation through strategic investments in ecosystem projects, and orchestrating global community engagement, KEF drives Kaspa's real-world adoption across finance, infrastructure, AI, energy, and retail."

## Activities

- Research and development funding
- Strategic investments in ecosystem projects
- Global community engagement
- Real-world adoption across finance, infrastructure, AI, energy, retail
- Grant programs (developer/research, partnership, event sponsorship)

## Featured projects funded by KEF

- **KasBay** - central hub for Kaspa services, tools, applications
- **Kasplex** - non-profit infrastructure layer for KRC-20 tokens, NFTs
- **KasKeeper** - secure Kaspa wallet (Android via Google Play)
- **Research & Grants** - cryptographic research funding

## Investor takeaways

- KEF exists as a real entity making real grants - cited by XT.com as "planning to lead investment in KRC-20 DEX and wallet construction"
- Legal jurisdiction and structure of KEF is **not disclosed on the public homepage** (CMC says "no central governance or business model" - tension)
- Two parallel foundations (KEF + Kaspa Kii) suggests fragmented institutional layer, not a single accountable entity
- KEF clearly distinct from Kaspa core development (Sutton, Newman, IzioDev, etc.) - core remains community-led; KEF is supporter/grantmaker
- The leadership names (Junny, Evie, Effy) are publicly active but appear to be pseudonyms / handles on X - need diligence on legal-name accountability
