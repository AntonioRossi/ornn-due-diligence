## Investor-relevant takeaways

- The fair-launch / no-premine / no-insider-allocation narrative is structurally embedded in the project's marketing - a credibility differentiator vs L1s with foundation allocations or VC tranches.
- The genesis proof is independently reproducible via published code (Python notebook in `kaspagang/kaspad-py-explorer`).
- 10 BPS is presented as a current, delivered capability, not a roadmap item (Crescendo hard fork activated May 2025).
