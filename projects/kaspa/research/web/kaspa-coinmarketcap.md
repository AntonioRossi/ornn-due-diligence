## Project description (CMC editorial)

- Fast, scalable Layer-1 cryptocurrency built on proof-of-work (PoW)
- Powered by **GHOSTDAG protocol** - novel consensus extending Nakamoto's design
- Allows parallel blocks to coexist; orders within a Directed Acyclic Graph (blockDAG)
- Currently 10 BPS post-Crescendo upgrade
- Long-term goal: 100 BPS

## Founders / organization

- **Originator: Dr. Yonatan Sompolinsky** (Harvard University)
- Research conducted under Prof. **Aviv Zohar** (Hebrew University)
- Project began through **DAGLabs** (research company founded by Sompolinsky, funded by Polychain Capital)
- **Elichai Turkel** (Bitcoin Core contributor) served as DAGLabs' applied cryptographer
- **DAGLabs dissolved around the time of Kaspa's fair launch in November 2021**, transitioning project to decentralized, community-led model
- "Kaspa remains an open-source, permissionless project with no central governance or business model."
