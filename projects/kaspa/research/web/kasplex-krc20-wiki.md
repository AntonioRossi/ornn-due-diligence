## What Kasplex is

- 3rd-party group's solution on top of Kaspa L1
- Implements data insertion protocol, open-source indexer, data availability mechanisms, APIs
- Introduces **KRC-20 protocol** - standard for creating tokens and NFTs on Kaspa

## Investor takeaways

- KRC-20 is the de facto Kaspa token standard, but currently inscription-style (off-chain indexing)
- Native KRC-20 consensus support comes with the Toccata hard fork - lifts dependence on third-party indexers
- Significant wallet/tooling ecosystem already exists, suggesting demand is real (vs purely speculative protocol)
- Notable: third-party group operates the indexer/protocol, not the core Kaspa team - introduces counterparty risk for current KRC-20 holders that should be reduced after Toccata makes native KRC-20 a first-class L1 concept
