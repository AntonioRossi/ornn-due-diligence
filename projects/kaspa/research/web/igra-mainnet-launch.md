# Igra Network Launches Public Mainnet (Chainwire via TradingView, Mar 19 2026)

Source: https://www.tradingview.com/news/chainwire:48fbb241f094b:0-igra-network-launches-public-mainnet-as-decentralized-evm-layer-on-kaspa-s-proof-of-work-blockdag/
Published: March 19, 2026, 14:12 GMT
Distribution: Chainwire press release (Zug, Switzerland)
Scraped: 2026-05-27

## Headline claims

- Public access opened to "3,000+ TPS smart contract environment secured by proof-of-work consensus"
- "Following six months of testing with zero state divergence"
- **Fifteen protocols deploying at launch**
- Cross-chain via Hyperlane
- **Security audit by Sigma Prime completed with no unresolved issues**

## Testnet metrics

- 730,000+ transactions across 21 million blocks
- Zero state divergence

## Kaspa context (Igra Labs' own framing)

- Kaspa market cap "nearing $1 billion"
- 500,000+ active addresses
- **$486 million trading volume on day KRC-20 token protocol functionality launched** - cited as evidence of latent demand
- "Despite that demand, the ecosystem has operated with less than $1 million in DeFi total value locked due to the absence of a decentralized and programmable smart contract layer"
- Igra Network is positioned as the closing-of-the-gap solution

## Architecture summary

- Based rollup: "transaction ordering is delegated entirely to the base layer rather than handled by a centralized sequencer"
- "Kaspa miners sequence Igra transactions without the ability to read their contents"
- "Resistance to MEV extraction, front-running, and transaction censorship at the protocol level rather than as an application-layer patch"
- "Over 3,000 transactions per second with sub-second inclusion latency"
- "Sigma Prime, the firm behind Ethereum's Lighthouse consensus client, completed clean with no unresolved issues"

## Launch protocols (15 named)

- **Kaskad** (Aave V3-style lending)
- **ZealousSwap** (Uniswap v2 DEX)
- **Zealous Auctions Protocol (ZAP)** (Continuous Clearing Auctions for token launches)
- **Hyperlane** (cross-chain + USDC.e bridging)
- **Kasperia**, **Kasware** (wallets)
- **KAT Bridge** (KRC-20 token + KRC-721 NFT bridging)
- **Dagscan** (block explorer)
- **Kaspa.com** (DEX + launchpad)
- Additional protocols (total 15)

## TVL

- "Ecosystem partners collectively manage **over $5 million in total value locked** across the Kaspa ecosystem"

## iKAS bridge mechanic

- Native KAS wraps 1:1 to **iKAS** on Igra via trust-minimized bridge backed by locked KAS on L1
- iKAS is the network's gas token

## H2 2026 plans

- Second-generation execution engine with **Block-STM parallel processing**
- **Agent-native infrastructure** for machine-to-machine payment, identity, orchestration

## Quotes (verbatim)

> Pavel Emdin (CEO, Igra Labs): "There is over a billion dollars in ecosystem value on Kaspa and $486 million in volume on a single day when KRC-20 launched, yet almost no sufficiently decentralized programmable infrastructure exists to capture it. That gap is now closed."

> Ashton Wood (Head of Ecosystem & Business Development): "Fifteen teams committed before mainnet went live. Hyperlane gives us cross-chain connectivity and stablecoin access from day one, and Kaskad brings institutional-grade lending to proof-of-work for the first time."

- **Swiss association** governance

## IGRA auction details

- Late March 2026 via **ZAP** (Zealous Auctions Protocol)
- Same mechanism as **Aztec's $59M sale**
- On-chain price discovery, no lockup or vesting on auctioned supply, tokens fully liquid on claim
- $0.006 floor, 3.5% of supply
- Open to anyone with iKAS on network
- Secondary trading via ZealousSwap DEX

## Investor takeaways

- **Sigma Prime audit is a major credibility marker** - same firm built Lighthouse, Ethereum's most-used consensus client
- "$486M one-day volume on KRC-20 launch + sub-$1M DeFi TVL" is a strong gap narrative - real demand without programmable infrastructure
- 6 months of zero-divergence testnet operation = mainnet readiness
- Sale mechanism explicitly compares to Aztec's $59M raise - frames the IGRA auction as a proven institutional auction format
- The "agent-native infrastructure" pivot (H2 2026) tracks the AI agent narrative gaining institutional traction
- iKAS bridge is trust-minimized but still a bridge - a 2026-shape risk vector worth diligencing
