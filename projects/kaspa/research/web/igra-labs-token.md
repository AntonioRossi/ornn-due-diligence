## Token utilities

1. **Attesting**: "Attesters ensure Igra-to-Kaspa state consistency and receive protocol-level incentives and fee participation."
2. **Governance via Igra DAO**:
   - Attestation rules
   - Rewards and penalties calibration
   - Bridge configuration and updates
   - Allocation of ecosystem grants
3. **Swiss Compliance**: "The Igra Association, which develops the Igra ecosystem and its $IGRA utility token, is registered in Switzerland, ensuring clear legal responsibility and transparent management of funds."
4. **Future utility**: securing bridges, additional execution environments, preferential access to network services

## Investor takeaways

- 10% float at TGE is moderate - not as tight as some launches, not as loose as airdrops
- **Team allocation (18%) is reasonable**, has 6-mo lockup + 36-mo vesting - aligned long-term
- 47% of supply (Ecosystem + Community) is under "phased DAO control" with 60-month linear vesting - very long emission curve, moderate dilution per quarter
- Association allocation (20%) funds Swiss entity operations - similar to other foundation-controlled supply
- Public Token Sale (5%) was the IGRA auction concluded March 28 – April 3, 2026 at 0.1652 iKAS via ZealousSwap ZAP (528 unique bidders, 49.36M IGRA sold)
- **No standalone VC/seed tranche disclosed** - this is unusual and notable; aligns with "no hidden actors" claim
- Fixed supply + utility-driven demand framework
