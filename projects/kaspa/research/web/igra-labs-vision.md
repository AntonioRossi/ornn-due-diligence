# Igra Labs Vision

Source: https://igralabs.com/vision
Scraped: 2026-05-27

## Endgame statement (verbatim)

> "Igra's endgame is to deliver a platform that combines Bitcoin-grade security with the versatility of modern programmable chains. We aim to unify any kind of decentralized VMs under one roof, making them secure, ultra-fast, MEV-resistant and interoperable."

## Critique of incumbent paradigms

- **PoS L1s**: "deadlocked by compromises made in terms of scalability, decentralization, and security... inevitable consequence of choosing PoS as the core consensus principle and settling for a technical roadmap that promotes endless fragmentation"
- **Bitcoin ZK rollups**: "native ZK verification on Bitcoin lacks a clear timeline. Even when implemented, these rollups can only use Bitcoin's base layer for settlement, lacking data availability and sequencing capabilities"
- **Ethereum rollup-centric**: "trapped its ecosystem in iterative prisoner's dilemma games, resulting in fragmented liquidity, limited scaling, and distorted economic incentives for base layer development"

## Why Kaspa specifically

- "Kaspa BlockDAG represents an attempt to surpass the limits of Nakamoto consensus."
- "By establishing transaction ordering at L1 and deterministically deriving the L2 state directly from the base layer, it eliminates the need for additional consensus mechanisms."

## Phase 1 target

- L2 on Kaspa with canonical L1↔L2 bridge
- **Up to 3,000 TPS with sub-second finality**
- EVM-compatible, seamless use of existing Ethereum tools
- MEV-resistance via Kaspa L1 scalable leaderless DAG design with intra-round mechanism

## Long-term composability

- Leverages Kaspa's planned synchronous atomic composability
- "Igra will enable interoperability between systems with any type of VM—whether Solana VM, Move, or WASM."
- Goal: "smart contracts to interact seamlessly across different L2s, regardless of which virtual machine they use"
- "Specialized smart contracts in different languages can be composed into powerful meta-dApps"

## Name etymology

- "Igra" in Aramaic means "Rooftop"
- Positioning: "unifying layer under which different decentralized virtual machines can thrive"

## Investor takeaways

- Vision is intentionally bigger than EVM-on-Kaspa - it aspires to be a multi-VM unification layer
- Phase 1 (EVM rollup at 3K TPS) is achievable; Phase 2 (multi-VM atomic composability) is research-stage and depends on Kaspa's vProgs
- The critique of PoS and Ethereum rollups is explicit - this is positioning for PoW-aligned capital, not generalist crypto allocators
- Founders' personal investment thesis is technical, not narrative-driven - aligns with engineer-founder credibility profile
