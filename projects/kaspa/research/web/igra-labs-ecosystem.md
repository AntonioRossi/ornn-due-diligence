Source: https://igralabs.com/ecosystem

## Ecosystem participants (mainnet vs testnet)

### Mainnet

- **Zealous Swap** (DEX): Kaspa's first AMM DEX with NFT-based fees, POL, insurance fund, modular fees
- **Tangem** (hardware wallet): card form factor, NFC-signed
- **Zelcore** (wallet): non-custodial multi-platform
- **Kasperia** (wallet): "Ultra-light Kaspa wallet"
- **Kastle** (wallet): Kaspa wallet
- **Kaspa.com** (DeFi platform): DEX, lending & borrowing, launchpad, NFTs (community-run)
- **Kasware** (wallet): KRC20, KNS, KRC721, L2 network support
- **Hyperlane** (cross-chain messaging): permissionless interoperability layer, 100+ chains via warp routes
- **Quex** (oracle): TEE-based confidential computing, intelligent data oracle
- **GS Compliance** (AI compliance): regulatory compliance for energy/commodity trading
- **DagScan** (block explorer)
- **Nacho the Kat** (memecoin): largest fair-launched memecoin on Kaspa

### Testnet

- **Moonbound** (token launchpad)
- **Kyo** (DeFi lending + card)
- **Spectre Market** (NFT marketplace)
- **Kaskad** (lending protocol; Aave V3-style)
- **Aporia** (CLOB DEX)
- **Fervent Finance** (lending: risk-isolated pools)
- **Kaspa Finance** (DeFi super protocol: V3-style AMMs, lending, launchpad, AI bots)
- **Knexous** (Tap2Pay NFC payment platform)

### Community partners / dev

- **KAT Alliance / KAT Foundation** (transparency advocacy)
- **KaspaPulse** (community news; explicitly not an official entity)
- **Crest Development** (development studio)
- **TBDai** (business directory, 32M+ on-chain B2B platform)

## Investor takeaways

- 20+ live or testnet protocols within 2 months of mainnet launch - meaningful ecosystem density
- DEX (ZealousSwap) is live on mainnet; lending and NFT marketplaces are still on testnet - DeFi vertical maturity is uneven
- Cross-chain liquidity flow via Hyperlane is the most important enabler - reduces Kaspa's historical "isolated chain" handicap
- Quex oracle in TEE adds privacy + confidential computing primitives
- Memecoin and DEX-first launch profile mirrors typical early L2 patterns (low cold-start friction, then DeFi maturity)
- The presence of **Kaspa.com** as a separate community-run super-app suggests the brand is being captured by entrepreneurs; structural risk for naming/branding clarity
- No tier-1 institutional protocols (Aave native, MakerDAO, etc.) yet - this is normal but slows institutional adoption flywheel
