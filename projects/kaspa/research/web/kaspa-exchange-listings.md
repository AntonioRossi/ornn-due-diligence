## Investor takeaways

- **KAS is in mid-stage exchange adoption**: present on Tier 2/3 exchanges, absent from Tier 1 US-regulated venues
- No confirmed Binance/Coinbase listing date as of March 2026
- Listing on Tier 1 venues would likely be a meaningful liquidity catalyst, but is not a near-term datable event
- The Kraken Nov 2024 listing is widely reported by Kaspa community but Bitget contradicts it - check kraken.com directly for confirmation in diligence
- For institutional allocation: liquidity exits via Bitget/KuCoin/MEXC require additional venue diligence (proof of reserves, compliance posture, jurisdictional risk)
