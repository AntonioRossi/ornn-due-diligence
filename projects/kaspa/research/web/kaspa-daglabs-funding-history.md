## Founding

- **DAGLabs founded 2017** by **Yonatan Sompolinsky** (then PhD candidate, Hebrew University) and **Guy Corem**
- Headquartered Israel; later Sompolinsky moved to Harvard for postdoc
- Initial mission: research and develop the GhostDAG protocol; explore optical proof-of-work (OPoW) ASICs

## Funding

- **Total raised: ~$8 million**
- **Lead: Polychain Capital**
- **Co-investor: Accomplice**
- Funding structure was R&D contract, not equity in Kaspa the cryptocurrency
- ByteTree: "Polychain Capital primarily funded the project following an initial $8m funding round as a vote of confidence. DAGLabs repaid Polychain Capital..."

## Critical fact: DAGLabs repaid Polychain

- ByteTree confirms DAGLabs **repaid** Polychain Capital
- This is the structural basis for Kaspa's "no premine, no VC tranche, fair launch" claim
- Wiki: "PolyChain paid $8,000,000 to rent the brainpower of the above team for over 3 years to research and develop Kaspa. Every block, every second, has $8,000,000 of [development] backing"

## DAGLabs dissolution

- DAGLabs dissolved around the time of Kaspa's fair launch (November 7, 2021)
- Transition to decentralized, community-led model
- Sompolinsky and other ex-DAGLabs personnel continued contributing to Kaspa as community participants (no formal entity)

## Ex-DAGLabs personnel still in ecosystem

- **Michael Sutton**: core developer, author of Crescendo and Toccata roadmap posts
- **Ori Newman**: initiated Toccata covenants work
- **Elichai Turkel**: applied cryptographer at DAGLabs, Bitcoin Core contributor (currently no public Kaspa role)
- **Mike Zak**: core teamlead @ DAGLabs, now Blockchain Developer @ Igra Labs
- **Denis Mashkevich**: teamlead @ DAGLabs, now Chief of Strategy @ Igra Labs
- **Roman Melnikov**: Chief Architect @ Kaspa/DAGLabs, now advisor to Igra Labs

## Investor takeaways

- The $8M Polychain investment was a **research grant, not an equity stake**, and was **repaid**. There is no surviving VC capitalization table for Kaspa the cryptocurrency.
- Fair launch claim is structurally defensible: every KAS was mined under PoW; Polychain held no tokens by allocation.
- The professional/institutional connection between Igra Labs and original DAGLabs personnel (Zak, Mashkevich, Melnikov) gives Igra a unique "Kaspa OG team migration" story.
- Sompolinsky's continued advocacy (Oxford Union address, Tokenize keynotes) provides ongoing thought-leadership without formal entity attachment.
- For a diligence team, the absence of a surviving entity = upside (no foundation slush risk) AND downside (no entity to accountability-test).
