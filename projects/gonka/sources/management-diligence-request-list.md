# Management diligence request list — Gonka / Product Science Inc. (GNK)

Tailored to **Gonka / Product Science Inc. / the Gonka network (GNK token)** as of **May 26, 2026** and based on public information only. The public record — a working Cosmos SDK–style mainnet, a $50M Bitfury investment, a CertiK-audited code base with no Critical findings, ~11,000 H100-equivalent self-reported compute scale, and a credible serial-founder team (the Liberman siblings, prior Snap acquisition) — is sufficient to justify deeper diligence, but not sufficient to underwrite a token purchase, treasury allocation, or equity-equivalent exposure. The gating issues are: **(1) the absent legal-entity map between Product Science Inc., the Gonka network, and any successor foundation/DAO, (2) US and non-US securities-law classification for a hybrid PoW + collateral + Work-Coin + founder-allocation token, (3) the 200M GNK founders' allocation (20% of supply) with no public vesting or lock-up schedule, (4) effectively zero exchange liquidity ($0 of 24-hour volume against a $0.26 reference price, ~90% drawdown from a Jan 2026 $2.61 high) and the price-discovery integrity of the community-pool sale mechanism, (5) operational definitions and independent verification for every "active node / developer / token throughput" traction figure, and (6) control-function disclosure (general counsel, chief compliance officer, head of security, head of finance) and named institutional customer references.**

## Management diligence request list

**Instruction to management:** please provide the following in a dataroom, with one folder owner per section, latest revision date on each file, and a short note indicating whether the file is final, draft, or board-only. Where a document does not exist, please say so explicitly rather than substituting a marketing or whitepaper equivalent.

**Diligence sequencing:** populate all **P0 / gating** items first. No token purchase, equity investment, or treasury allocation should be considered until the P0 package is substantially complete, management interviews are held, and an independent on-chain audit of traction and treasury flows is finished.

### A. Corporate, legal, and regulatory (P0 / gating)

1. **Full legal entity chart and ownership map**

   * For each entity: jurisdiction of incorporation, formation date, registered office, directors/managers, beneficial ownership (including the Liberman siblings' individual stakes), employees, bank accounts, revenue booking entity, IP ownership, and all intercompany agreements.
   * Specifically address: which entity (i) holds the gonka.ai domain registration, (ii) owns the protocol IP and the open-source repository at github.com/gonka-ai, (iii) signed the Bitfury investment documents, (iv) holds custody of the 200M Founders' GNK allocation, (v) holds the operational private keys for the community-pool smart contract.
   * Reconcile the apparent contradiction between the December 2025 Bitfury press release ("no foundation or similar entity was established to gatekeep [the network's] evolution") and the continued operational role of Product Science Inc. (current media contact `andrey@productscience.ai`, founder authorship of the whitepaper, CTO-of-Product-Science title still used in the press release).

2. **Token classification and securities-law analysis (US)**

   * Outside-counsel written memo addressing GNK's classification under US federal securities laws, with explicit treatment of each of the following five reward/distribution mechanisms separately rather than collectively:
     - (a) **Epoch-minted Reward Coins** distributed by PoC weight
     - (b) **Work Coins** paid by Developers for inference work
     - (c) **Utilization bonuses** and **model coverage incentives**
     - (d) The **200M Founders' Allocation**
     - (e) The **Community Pool sale mechanism** (preprogrammed USDT/ETH/BTC → GNK via native bridge)
   * Address whether each mechanism falls within the SEC Division of Corporation Finance Staff Statement on Certain Proof-of-Work Mining Activities (March 20, 2025), and identify which mechanisms are **outside the safe harbor** and on what alternative theory they are not investment contracts under *Howey*.
   * Address whether the **collateral-backed influence** feature (Hosts lock GNK to unlock 80% of governance weight) is staking, and if so, how it interacts with the Staff Statement's PoW-only scope.
   * Confirm whether GNK has ever been sold or made available to US persons through any mechanism (community pool, bounty, airdrop, OTC desk, named partner sale, exchange listing), and provide copies of all SAFT, SAFE, token-warrant, or similar instruments.
   * Identify any prior SEC, CFTC, FinCEN, OFAC, or state-AG inquiries, subpoenas, or no-action requests relating to GNK or Product Science Inc.

3. **Non-US securities and crypto-asset analysis**

   * Outside-counsel memos for each of: **EU (MiCA)**, **UK (FCA)**, **Switzerland (FINMA)**, **Singapore (MAS)**, **ADGM (FSRA)**, **DIFC**, **Canada (CSA)**, **Hong Kong (SFC)**, **UAE (VARA)**, and any other jurisdiction in which Gonka is materially marketed, has Hosts, or has Developers.
   * Particularly address: (i) MiCA classification of GNK (utility token, asset-referenced token, e-money token, or other), (ii) FCA's "qualifying cryptoasset" framework, (iii) whether the **community-pool preprogrammed sale mechanism** constitutes an ongoing public offering in any of these jurisdictions.

4. **Bitfury investment documentation**

   * Full set of investment documents for the **$12M Strategic round (Nov 26–27, 2025)** and the **$50M commitment announced Dec 2, 2025**, including: subscription agreements, token purchase agreements, side letters, registration-rights / information-rights agreements, governance-rights documents, and any token-warrant or SAFT-equivalent instruments.

What is the breakdown of the remaining ~$38M between (i) further GNK purchases, (ii) ecosystem grants, (iii) equity in Product Science Inc., (iv) development services, (v) marketing/business-development support?

   * For Product Science Inc., the Liberman siblings individually, any Gonka-related entity, and Bitfury (only as counterparty to Gonka): all current and prior litigation, arbitration, enforcement actions, regulatory inquiries (formal or informal), administrative subpoenas, and government investigations.

1. **Walk us through every legal entity in the Gonka stack today, who owns the equity in each, who controls the keys to each on-chain treasury, and who signed the Bitfury investment documents.**
2. **Which of Gonka's reward mechanisms do you believe fall within the SEC's March 20, 2025 PoW Staff Statement, which do not, and what is your legal position for the ones that do not — including the Work Coins, the collateral-backed influence model, the Founders' Allocation, and the Community Pool sale mechanism?**
3. **What is the vesting schedule for the 200M Founders' GNK allocation, and how much has any one Liberman sibling sold or transferred to date?**
4. **The Bitfury press release says no foundation exists. Product Science Inc. is still your operational center, and the whitepaper is signed by David Liberman with a gmx.com email and no entity. Help us reconcile.**
5. **The CertiK audit shows one Centralization (Privilege) finding acknowledged but unresolved. What is the role, and who controls it?**
6. **Give us three named institutional Developer references we can call this week, with monthly inference token volume.**
7. **GNK shows $0 of 24-hour volume on CoinMarketCap at $0.26, but the all-time high was $2.61 in January. What happened between January and now, and how does the community-pool sale mechanism currently set the reference price?**
8. **The whitepaper says cost-savings vs. centralized inference are "50% or more during the network's initial growth phase." George Kikvadze writes the current cost to mine a GNK is approximately $1, against a $0.26 market price. Are your Hosts profitable today, and what is your sensitivity analysis on Host churn if GNK stays at $0.26?**
9. **Who, by name and title, is your General Counsel, Chief Compliance Officer, and Head of Security today?**
10. **What is the highest-probability scenario in which a US, EU, or UK regulator concludes within the next 24 months that GNK is a security or otherwise restricted asset, and what is your mitigation plan?**
