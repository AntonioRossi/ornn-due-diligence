# Investment committee memo — Gonka / Product Science Inc. (GNK token)

**To:** Investment Committee
**From:** [Analyst]
**Date:** May 26, 2026
**Re:** **Gonka (GNK) — preliminary IC memo (public-information based)**

### Recommendation

**Authorize gated phase-two diligence only; do not approve an investment, token purchase, or treasury allocation yet.** The public record shows a credible serial-founder team (Liberman siblings; prior Snap acquisition), a substantive technical design with published whitepaper and CertiK code audit, real on-chain traction (~11,000 H100-equivalent compute, ~100M AI tokens/day across nine open-weight LLMs), and a marquee strategic backer in Bitfury ($50M committed, of which $12M was a community-pool token purchase). However, the evidence base remains heavily project-attributed and announcement-driven, and **six structural issues are unresolved**: **(1) the absence of a disclosed legal entity standing behind GNK, the whitepaper, and the founder allocation, (2) US and non-US securities-law classification for a hybrid PoW + collateral + Work-Coin + founder-allocation token, (3) the founders' 200M GNK allocation (20% of supply) with no public vesting or distribution schedule, (4) effectively zero exchange liquidity (24h volume $0; ~90% drawdown from a $2.61 January 2026 high), (5) operational definitions and independent verification for all "active node / developer / token throughput" traction figures, and (6) governance, control-function, and customer-reference disclosure gaps that would be standard for an institutional underwriting.** Until these can be resolved through direct management diligence and qualified legal opinion, no investment decision is supportable.

### Company snapshot

Gonka is a **decentralized network for AI inference and training** built on a Cosmos SDK–style Layer 1 blockchain. The network's stated thesis is that **"AI Compute is the New Currency"** [1] — that GPU compute should be tokenized and traded permissionlessly, with Bitcoin's incentive design repurposed so that nearly 100% of contributed compute is directed at productive AI workloads rather than synthetic consensus tasks. The native token is **GNK**, base on-chain unit `ngonka` (1 gonka = 1,000,000,000 ngonka) [2].

The network was **incubated by Product Science Inc.**, a Los Angeles–based US software company [3][4]. Product Science was founded by the **Liberman siblings — Anna, David, Daniil, and Maria** [4], serial entrepreneurs whose prior venture **Kernel AR was acquired by Snap Inc. (reportedly for $60M)** [3]. David Liberman is publicly described as **"CTO of Product Science Inc. and co-creator of the Gonka protocol"** [4] and is **the sole named author of the canonical whitepaper at gonka.ai/whitepaper.pdf**, listed with a `liberman@gmx.com` email and no corporate entity attached [5]. **The whitepaper bears no date, no version number, no copyright, no jurisdictional notice, no securities disclaimer, no audit reference, and names no legal entity** [5]. This is a material disclosure gap relative to institutional norms and is addressed in Core Risk #1.

**Mainnet launched September 15–16, 2025** per Dealroom and CertiK's milestone tracker (Bitfury's press release uses the informal "August 2025" framing) [3][6][4]. As of late May 2026, public sources report the network has scaled to **approximately 11,000 H100-equivalent compute** [7], processes **~100 million AI tokens per day across nine governance-approved open-weight models** [8][9], and shows **~600 active nodes from 30+ countries** [8] alongside **~15,400 Discord members and ~17,650 X/Twitter followers** [6]. All traction figures are **self-attributed via gonkascan.com**, the project-affiliated explorer; no independent third-party verification of the compute or throughput is available in the public record.

The network supports nine open-weight LLMs added through governance: **DeepSeek-R1, DeepSeek-V3, Gemma-3-27B, gpt-oss-120b, Kimi-k2.6, Llama-3.1-70B, Llama-3.1-405B, Qwen3-32B, and Qwen3-235B** [9]. There is **no first-party Gonka-trained model**; the network is, today, a permissionless inference layer for third-party open-weight models. Eligible hardware is **NVIDIA-only**, with a 40 GB VRAM-per-MLNode floor; supported GPUs range from H200 down to paired RTX 4090/3090 [10].

GNK is **listed on CoinMarketCap (UCID 39328, rank #7957) at $0.2598** as of May 26, 2026 [11]. **24-hour volume is $0; the token is not listed on any major centralized or decentralized exchange** [11][6]. The all-time high is **$2.61 (Jan 15, 2026)**, putting the current price ~**90% below ATH** [11]. The CertiK Skynet platform reports a **Skynet Score of 84.17 / A** with three completed audits (Ethereum Bridge, Inference, Consensus), no Critical findings, and all Major/Medium findings resolved, but with **one Centralization (Privilege) finding acknowledged-but-unresolved** and **no team KYC, no bug bounty** [6].

### What appears real today

The following items are dated, sourced, and triangulated across at least two independent sources or are visible directly on-chain or via the CertiK security platform. They are not "investor-friendly framing"; they are the floor under any subsequent thesis.

- **Working mainnet and code base.** Gonka shipped mainnet on Sep 15–16, 2025, and has run continuous **v0.x.x upgrades through to v0.2.9 (Feb 1, 2026)**, including activation of "**Proof of Contribution v2**" [6]. The open-source repo at github.com/gonka-ai/gonka shows real engineering activity (524+ stars; CertiK-driven fix commits visible in releases) [6].
- **Marquee strategic capital.** **Bitfury committed $50M** in a Dec 2, 2025 BusinessWire release [4], following a Nov 26–27 $12M Strategic round also from Bitfury Capital [6]. Per the press release and Kikvadze's Medium post, of the $50M, **$12M is identified as a Bitfury purchase of GNK from the community pool, approved by on-chain governance** [12][4]. The investment is part of Bitfury's separately announced **$1 billion ethical-technologies initiative** [4]. Bitfury Group brings credible Bitcoin-infrastructure history (founded 2011; ASIC mining heritage).
- **Network growth trajectory.** Compute scale, as self-reported by the project's explorer, has grown from 5,000 H100-eq (Nov 19, 2025) → 6,000 (Dec 2) → 10,729 (Dec 17) → ~11,000 (May 2026) [6][12][8][11]. This is a ~2x rise over six months — meaningful, though notably plateauing in 2026 relative to the Dec 2025 sprint.
- **CertiK audit posture.** Three modules audited (BLS-based Ethereum Bridge, inference, consensus). 58 total findings, **0 Critical, 4 Major (resolved), 10 Medium (resolved), 30 Minor (resolved), 13 Informational (5 acknowledged, 8 resolved), 1 Centralization (Privilege) acknowledged**. Most recent revision May 22, 2026 [6].
- **Open-source technical depth.** The whitepaper cites mainstream decentralized-ML research (DiLoCo, DiPaCo, GShard, Proof-of-Learning, federated learning primitives) [5]. The Transformer-based Sprint protocol is precisely specified (2.3B-parameter model; 1-in-900 difficulty target; honest-majority verification with 1–10% spot-check) [5]. This is not a vaporware design document; it is a working systems paper at research-engineering level.
- **Active inference workload.** Bitget/PANews report ~100M tokens/day total, with **Qwen3-235B-Instruct alone at ~30M tokens/day** as of Dec 17, 2025 [8]. If accurate, this places Gonka in the same order of magnitude as small commercial inference providers for a single high-tier model — directional but unaudited.
- **Cosmos SDK / IBC ecosystem integration.** The chain uses Cosmos-style validator addresses (`gonkavaloper1…`), is supported by Cosmos-ecosystem wallets (Keplr, Leap, Fox), and runs Ping Dashboard explorer infrastructure [11][13]. Governance terminology (Quorum 33.4%, NoWithVeto 33.4%) is Cosmos SDK gov-module idiom [5].

### Competitive context

Decentralized AI compute is **a crowded category** with at least eight funded competitors [14], each tackling the problem with a different mechanism:

| Project | Native token | Key positioning |
|---|---|---|
| **Bittensor** | TAO | Subnet-based incentivized intelligence; ~$2.7B market cap (early 2026); stake-weighted Yuma Consensus [14]. |
| **io.net** | IO | GPU-aggregation marketplace; commercial focus on AI/ML inference and training [14]. |
| **Akash Network** | AKT | General-purpose decentralized cloud; broader than AI [14]. |
| **Render Network** | RNDR | GPU rendering origin; expanding into AI inference [14]. |
| **Aethir, Gensyn, Ocean Protocol, Morpheus, Cudox, Fetch.ai, Ritual** | — | Adjacent GPU rental, federated training, agent infrastructure [14]. |

Gonka's stated differentiation is the **Bitcoin-style PoW alignment of incentives** and the **rejection of stake-weighted governance with delegation**. The whitepaper's load-bearing competitive claim is that **"in systems like Bittensor, 60% of rewards are allocated to staking … and the remaining 40% of rewards are also used inefficiently, as contributors to AI computation often repeat tasks to ensure accuracy"** [5]. The 60/40 number is not sourced inside the whitepaper and should be **independently stress-tested against Bittensor's current emission documentation** before being relied on in an investment thesis.

The Libermans' interview-stage comparison ("**Bittensor only has about 5,000 data center-grade GPUs**" [15]) is **unverified and likely cherry-picked**; Bittensor's actual subnet GPU count varies by subnet and methodology.

Independent third-party research flagged by Forbes is more cautious: **Galaxy Research (2025)** concluded decentralized networks can outperform centralized clouds **in specific workloads**, but **"warned that verification and reliability remain steep hurdles"** [16]. EPOCH AI describes the "paradox of distributed systems" — the more open they become, the more coordination they demand [16]. These critiques apply to Gonka and to every competitor.

For the multi-project comparison frame this firm uses across Ornn, Silicon Data, and Auctionomics, **Gonka sits in a different sub-segment**: those three projects are building **financial-market infrastructure for compute** (indices, derivatives, auction-based price discovery for GPU-rental contracts). Gonka is building **the supply layer itself** — the network that delivers inference. The two layers are complementary rather than substitutable: a successful Gonka could itself be a reference point for the indices Ornn/Silicon Data publish, and could be a venue Auctionomics-style auction designs could clear into.

### Team and operating-model risk

The **founders are the Liberman siblings** — Anna, David, Daniil, and Maria — with David publicly fronting the technical narrative [4][12][16]. Their public track record includes:
- **Kernel AR** — AR avatars for Snapchat; **acquired by Snap Inc. for ~$60M** (~2018); David and Daniil were subsequently Directors of Product at Snap [15][11].
- **Product Science Inc.** — AI-driven code-performance optimization platform with disclosed enterprise customers including **Walmart, JPMorgan Chase, and Airbnb** [15]. Product Science raised an **$18M round in 2023 from Coatue Management, Slow Ventures, and K5** [3][15]. Note: the $18M is a **Product-Science-level** raise, not a Gonka-direct raise; community-promotional sources ("$80M+ funding") conflate the two [17].
- **Frank Money** — earlier venture by the brothers [3].

Beyond David, the Gonka team is **not publicly disclosed**. Dealroom reports **11–50 employees** for Gonka AI [3]; no org chart, no general counsel, no chief compliance officer, no head of security, no head of finance is named in any public source. **No control-function hires** (legal, compliance, risk, treasury) are visible in the public record. For a token-issuing platform soliciting institutional capital exposure, this is a material gap (P0 in the diligence list).

**Specific operating-model risks:**
- **Anonymous-looking whitepaper.** The canonical technical document at gonka.ai/whitepaper.pdf — the foundational disclosure document for any token investor — bears no corporate entity, no date, no legal disclaimer, and is signed by a single individual with a free `gmx.com` email [5]. This is below institutional standards and stands in contrast to the legally-aware tokenomics PDF (which carries explicit US-securities-classification language [2]). The mismatch suggests two documents written by different audiences (technical research vs. legal/marketing) and not reconciled.
- **Founder allocation (200M GNK / 20%) has no public vesting schedule.** The tokenomics PDF states the 200M founders' allocation exists and recognizes founders' contribution, but does not specify a cliff, vesting curve, or distribution mechanism [2]. At current price ($0.26), the nominal value is **~$52M**; at the Jan 2026 ATH ($2.61), **~$522M**. Material concentration risk.
- **Dual-narrative governance claim.** The Bitfury press release states **"all control passed to an on-chain self-governed mechanism. No foundation or similar entity was established to gatekeep its evolution"** [4]. CoinMarketCap, by contrast, states the project was **"incubated by … Product Science Inc."** and "development kicked off in 2024 in a stealth mode … completed in February 2025" [11]. The transition from Product-Science-controlled stealth development to "no entity gatekeeps the network" is a **claim about ownership transfer that is not documented in the public record** — there is no public document showing IP assignment, code license transfer, key custody handoff, or treasury transfer between Product Science and any successor.
- **Press contact is still `andrey@productscience.ai`** [4]. The continued operational link to Product Science contradicts the "no foundation, no entity" framing.

### Core risks (numbered)

**Risk #1 — Disclosed-legal-entity gap and signatory ambiguity.** No single public source identifies which legal entity owns the gonka.ai domain, holds the protocol IP, signed the $50M Bitfury investment agreement, controls the founders' 200M GNK allocation, or bears liability if the network is challenged regulatorily. The whitepaper is anonymously corporate [5]. The tokenomics PDF carries no signatory [2]. The BusinessWire release describes the network as community-governed with "no foundation" [4], yet Product Science Inc. remains the operational fulcrum (media contact, technical leadership, original IP). **Diligence must establish the actual legal map and obtain a binding written representation from the management group as to which entities own what.**

**Risk #2 — Securities-law classification across jurisdictions.** Gonka's tokenomics PDF explicitly acknowledges this risk verbatim: **"The model assumes compliance with decentralized network principles and thus, no classification as a security in the United States and other jurisdictions with similar approaches to crypto asset regulation. … There is no guarantee that regulators, now or in the future, will agree with the positions taken by the Gonka project regarding the classification, treatment, or operation of GNK coins."** [2]. The relevant US backdrop is the SEC Division of Corporation Finance Staff Statement of March 20, 2025, which clarified that **classic PoW mining of "Covered Crypto Assets" on public, permissionless networks does not constitute an offer and sale of securities** [18]. Gonka's mechanism, however, is materially more complex than classic Bitcoin-style PoW. It combines:

  (a) **Epoch-minted Reward Coins** — distributed by PoC weight, plausibly within the PoW Staff Statement safe harbor;
  (b) **Work Coins** — paid by Developers for inference services, structurally **service fees** rather than mining rewards;
  (c) **Collateral-backed influence** — Hosts must lock GNK to unlock 80% of governance weight [2][5], a **PoS-like mechanism** the PoW Staff Statement does not address;
  (d) **The 200M Founders' Allocation** — a pre-mined founder grant, outside any mining safe harbor;
  (e) **The Community Pool sale mechanism** — preprogrammed USDT/ETH/BTC → GNK minting via native bridge [2], which can be characterized as an ongoing token sale by the network.

  Commissioner Crenshaw's dissent and the SEC's explicit reservation that "where facts vary … the [Staff's] view as to whether the specific Mining Activity involves the offer and sale of a security may be different" [18] mean **the safe harbor is partial, conditional, and revocable under future SEC composition**. Non-US regimes (EU MiCA, UK FCA, MAS, ADGM, FSRA, China) apply different and often stricter analyses. **A qualified, written, jurisdiction-specific legal opinion is a P0 gating item.**

**Risk #3 — Founder allocation concentration and unvested supply.** 200M GNK / 20% of supply is allocated to founders [2]. The tokenomics document does **not** specify vesting, cliff, lock-up, or distribution schedule. At current market $0.26, nominal value is ~$52M; at the Jan 2026 all-time high, ~$522M. Combined with the **acknowledged centralization (privilege) finding** in CertiK's audit [6] and the absence of an independent foundation, this constitutes **material concentration risk** that would normally be remediated by an institutional-grade lock-up and disclosure schedule.

**Risk #4 — Effective market illiquidity and price-discovery integrity.** GNK is **not listed on any major CEX or DEX** [11][6]. CoinMarketCap shows **$0 of 24-hour trading volume** at a quoted price of $0.2598, with a brief CertiK-noted spike to **$14,000 of 24h volume on May 21, 2026** [6]. The reference price almost certainly originates from the **community-pool preprogrammed sale mechanism** (USDT-to-GNK conversion) described in the tokenomics PDF [2], not from competitive order-book discovery. The all-time high of $2.61 on Jan 15, 2026 → current $0.26 represents a **~90% drawdown** [11]. **Implications for any investor:** (i) GNK cannot be marked-to-market against a liquid two-sided book today; (ii) any institutional entry or exit would itself materially move the reference price; (iii) Bitfury's $12M community-pool purchase at an unknown reference price is the only large-print transaction visible.

**Risk #5 — Traction-claim verifiability.** Every headline traction figure in this memo is **self-attributed via gonkascan.com or the project's own dashboards** [7][8][11]. There is **no independent third-party verification** of the ~11,000 H100-equivalent compute, the ~100M tokens/day throughput, the ~600 active nodes, the ~2,200 developers, or the geographic distribution. The "active node" definition is not disclosed (uptime threshold? minimum work?), nor is developer counting methodology. **Diligence must obtain (a) operational definitions, (b) raw on-chain data export, (c) at least three named institutional customer references with disclosed monthly usage, (d) audit of the explorer's data pipeline.** Until these are obtained, all traction figures must be treated as **directional only**.

**Risk #6 — Competitive durability and host unit economics.** Two structural concerns:

  (a) **Host profitability at current GNK price.** Kikvadze's Medium post claims the **"current cost to mine a GNK token: approximately $1"** [12] versus the live $0.26 market price [11]. If accurate, Hosts are running at a fiat loss subsidized only by speculative belief in token-price recovery. This dynamic is structurally consistent with Bitcoin's early years but is **material to the network's near-term sustainability** — Host churn could cascade if token price stays depressed.

  (b) **Competitive durability.** Bittensor, io.net, Akash, Render, Aethir, Gensyn, and Ritual all operate in adjacent territory [14]. Gonka's differentiation rests on the **PoW-incentive-alignment thesis**, which is design-philosophical and **not yet demonstrably superior in measurable economic outcomes**. The Libermans' "ASIC revolution" analogy [12][15][16] is plausible but speculative — it took Bitcoin **roughly five years** (2009 → ~2014) to traverse the GPU-to-ASIC transition, and the AI-inference workload is **fundamentally different from hash puzzles** (it is data-dependent, non-deterministic, and model-specific). Custom AI-inference ASICs (Cerebras, Groq, Etched, Tenstorrent) exist and are **vertically integrated with centralized providers**, not with decentralized networks.

### Bottom line

Gonka is a **substantive technical and capital-formation story** with credible serial founders, real engineering output, a top-tier strategic backer in Bitfury, and a working mainnet at non-trivial scale. The project is materially differentiated from competitors in its consensus design and in its Bitcoin-style emission philosophy.

The reasons for the gated recommendation are **not technical**. They are **structural and institutional**: the absence of a disclosed legal entity, the unresolved securities-law posture across multiple jurisdictions, the lack of a public vesting schedule for a 20%-of-supply founder allocation, the effectively zero exchange liquidity, the absence of institutional control-function staffing, and the dependence on project-attributed traction data without independent verification. Each of these is solvable through diligence and disclosure; **none has been solved in the public record today**.

**The conclusion would change if** (i) management produces a binding legal-entity map, jurisdiction-specific securities opinions, and a vesting schedule for the founders' allocation; (ii) at least three named institutional customers disclose monthly inference usage that triangulates the ~100M tokens/day claim; (iii) GNK obtains tier-1 CEX listing with two-sided liquidity that validates the $200–300M enterprise-value estimate [3] against market discovery; (iv) the dual-narrative on Product-Science-vs-no-foundation governance is reconciled with documented IP, treasury, and key-custody transfers; and (v) named control-function hires (GC, CCO, head of security) are made and publicly disclosed.

Until then, **Gonka warrants further diligence, but not capital**.
