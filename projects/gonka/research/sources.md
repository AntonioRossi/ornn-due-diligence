## Reliability legend

- **Company-generated** = Gonka, Product Science, or Bitfury direct material
  (docs, PDFs, press releases). Treat factual content as **self-reported**.
- **Third-party** = independent press, data platforms, security platforms, law
  firm analysis. Treat as **secondary** — useful for triangulation but not
  immune to error.
- **Regulatory** = government / SEC / etc. — primary authority for regulatory
  questions.
- **Project-affiliated** = sources connected to the project ecosystem (explorers,
  community sites) — treat as **company-adjacent**.
