## Governance and structure

- Author claims: "fairly launched, with all control passed to an on-chain
  self-governed mechanism. **No foundation. No gatekeepers.**"
- Community pool: **120M GNK (12% of lifetime supply)** controlled by Hosts via
  governance.
- Bitfury's $12M GNK purchase from community pool was approved by transparent
  on-chain voting.

## Author's framing (PoW-vs-PoS thesis)

- Argues PoS networks "consume more than 90% of GPU cycles on consensus tasks";
  Gonka "flips this model: nearly 100% of contributed resources directed toward
  productive AI workloads."
- "Every GNK token is backed by measurable work, not speculation."

## Etymology

- "Gonka" = "race" in Russian.
- Tibetan Buddhist reference: "Gonkhang" / "Gön-khang" = Protector Chapel; gön
  = protector, khang = dwelling.
