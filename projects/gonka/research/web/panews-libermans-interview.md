## Founders' background (per their own account)

- Brothers, lives and careers intertwined since 2003.
- 2003: interest in parallel computing and decentralized networks.
- Online gaming → computer vision → AR avatars for **Snapchat**.
- **Snapchat acquired their company (Kernel AR) for $60M** (per Dealroom +
  context).
- After Snap, founded **Product Science** — AI code optimization service for
  **Walmart, JPMorgan Chase, Airbnb**.
- Product Science investors: **Coatue, K5, Slow Ventures**.

## Gonka founding narrative

- Started in 2024 in stealth as a passion project (per CoinMarketCap "About"
  section).
- Mainnet launched August/September 2025.
- Inspiration: Bitcoin-style infrastructure incentives applied to AI compute.
- Goal: replicate the GPU-to-ASIC efficiency revolution in AI inference
  hardware.

## Key competitive claims (made by founders in interview)

- "Even Bittensor, currently the largest decentralized AI computing network,
  only has about **5,000 data center-grade GPUs**."
- "OpenAI and xAI building massive clusters with millions of top-tier GPUs."
- "Approximately **65% of global cloud computing power is controlled by three
  American companies (AWS, Google Cloud, etc.). If we add China's Alibaba and
  Tencent, these five giants control a staggering 80% of global cloud computing
  power.**"
- "If we can replicate the innovative transformation of ASICs in the AI field,
  the world will be very different."

## Operational anecdotes (Hosts and data center participants)

- "**Gcore** and **Hyperfusion**" — named commercial data center operators that
  "have begun gradually migrating hundreds of GPUs from their rental business to
  our network" because Gonka yields are higher than their rental rates.
- "Some very successful AI startups bought hundreds of H200 GPUs with investor
  money during the early hype, but only half of them are being effectively
  utilized" — Libermans cite these as Gonka onboarding candidates.

## Sovereignty engagement (per founders)

- "A year ago … the governments of some countries [had] mainstream idea[s] of
  'we want to build our own clusters and create sovereign AI.'"
- "A year later, when we met with ministers from countries such as the **UAE
  and Kazakhstan**, they all clearly realized that independent players with a
  small number of GPUs simply could not compete with the giants."
- Gonka pitched to governments as a "trusted decentralized network" to preserve
  sovereignty.

## On the "AI bubble" question

- Founders distinguish "AI bubble" from "specific bet bubble" — argue the
  underlying AI shift is real but specific bets (e.g. Nvidia, hyperscalers)
  may be overvalued.
- "What would happen if the ASIC revolution were to repeat itself in the AI
  field? Imagine rebuilding the entire computing power of today's Bitcoin
  network using Nvidia's latest Blackwell chips instead of ASIC miners — you
  would need to invest $500 trillion!"
