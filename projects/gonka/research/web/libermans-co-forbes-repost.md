## Independent skeptical references cited (Forbes author)

- **Galaxy Research (2025)**: decentralized networks can outperform centralized
  clouds in specific workloads, "but warned that verification and reliability
  remain steep hurdles."
- **EPOCH AI**: "the paradox of distributed systems": the more open, the more
  coordination they demand.
- **Pew Research Center**: technology is "neither inherently helpful nor
  harmful. It is simply a tool… The real effects of technology depend upon how
  it is wielded."

## Notes for analyst

- Forbes piece is **mostly favorable** but includes independent-research caveats
  that the Libermans interview format doesn't. Verification and reliability of
  decentralized inference is a flagged open problem (Galaxy Research).
- "Intentionally avoided features like delegation" is an important architecture
  claim — in many PoS networks, delegation has driven validator/stake
  concentration. This claim should be verified against the actual chain
  governance code (the BLS module mentioned in the CertiK audit is the relevant
  surface).
- Sovereignty narrative (UAE, Kazakhstan mentioned by Libermans elsewhere) is
  reinforced by Forbes.
