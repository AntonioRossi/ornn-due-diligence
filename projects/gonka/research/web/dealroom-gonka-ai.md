## Description (Dealroom, AI-generated summary)

> Gonka AI operates a decentralized computing network for AI training and
> inference, designed as an alternative to centralized cloud providers. The
> platform allows anyone, from individuals with a single GPU to large-scale
> data centers, to contribute their hardware's computing power to the network.

> The company was founded by brothers David and Daniil Liberman, serial
> entrepreneurs who previously founded **Product Science Inc.**, **Kernel AR
> (acquired by Snap Inc.)**, and **Frank Money**.

> Gonka officially launched on **September 15, 2025**.

> The business model is built on a **Layer 1 blockchain** that uses a novel
> **Transformer-based Proof-of-Work mechanism called Sprint Consensus**. This
> system allocates voting weight based on verified computational work.

> Gonka has secured significant funding, including an **$18 million round in
> 2023** from investors like **Coatue Management** and **Slow Ventures**, and
> a subsequent **$12 million strategic investment from Bitfury Group** in
> November 2025.
