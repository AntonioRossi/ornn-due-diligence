- **Bitfury committed $50M to Gonka.**
- Investment is the **first commitment** under Bitfury's previously announced
  **$1 billion initiative** (announced Nov 17, 2025).
- "Designed to accelerate the development and adoption of the Gonka protocol
  and its ecosystem."

  Sept 15/16, 2025 launch date noted by CertiK and Dealroom — official "August"

  inference tasks."
