## Notable comparative observations from coverage

- The decentralized-compute sector is **crowded**, with at least 8–10
  funded competitors. Differentiation is on consensus mechanism (PoW vs PoS),
  workload type (training vs inference vs rendering), and tokenomics.
- Bittensor is the dominant token by market cap among AI-narrative crypto;
  Gonka explicitly compares itself to Bittensor on architecture (claiming
  PoW alignment vs Bittensor's stake-weighted approach).
- io.net, Akash, Render are general decentralized GPU marketplaces;
  Bittensor positions as protocol layer for AI-specific subnets.
- Gonka's differentiation thesis (per founders): replicate Bitcoin's
  "ASIC revolution" arc; bind tokens to verifiable inference work; reject
  staking/delegation features that lead to centralization in PoS systems.

## Notes for analyst

- For the **competitive context section** of the IC memo, claims about
  Bittensor's GPU count (~5,000) should be **flagged as founder-self-attributed**
  unless independently verified against Bittensor's subnet registry.
- Gonka's strongest competitive claim is **inference market specialization**
  with PoW alignment — io.net/Akash/Render do not use PoW for productive AI
  validation; Bittensor uses Yuma Consensus + stake weights.
- Sector-wide unresolved problems flagged by Galaxy Research and EPOCH AI (per
  Forbes article): verification reliability, coordination overhead in open
  networks. These apply to Gonka and competitors alike.
