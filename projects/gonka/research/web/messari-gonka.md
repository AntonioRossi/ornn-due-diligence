## Description (verbatim)

> Gonka is a decentralized network that provides GPU-based computing power for
> AI model training and inference. It connects hardware providers ("hosts") with
> developers, enabling anyone from individual GPU owners to data centers to
> contribute compute and be rewarded in the network's native token (GNK). Gonka
> operates under a "transformer-based Proof-of-Work / Sprint Consensus" protocol
> that allocates nearly 100% of compute power to meaningful AI workloads rather
> than generic blockchain tasks.
