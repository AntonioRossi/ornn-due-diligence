## CEO statement highlights (Kulkarni)

> "In the first quarter, we continued to execute on our strategy by bringing our 108-qubit Cepheus-1-108Q system into general availability on Rigetti QCS, Amazon Braket, Microsoft Azure Quantum, and qBraid."

> "We believe Cepheus-1-108Q is one of the most powerful generally available gate-based quantum computers in the world, and as the largest modular system on the market today, it is an important validation of our chiplet-based architecture in a production setting."

## Operational highlights

- **108-qubit Cepheus-1-108Q general availability** on Rigetti QCS, AWS Braket, Microsoft Azure Quantum, qBraid
- **Novera QPU sale** to University of Saskatchewan (9-qubit, on-premises)
- **Q1 fidelity progress**: median 99.8% two-qubit gate fidelity with 40ns gate speeds on 9-qubit system; 99.9% on prototype systems
- **$100M UK investment plan announced**: deploying >1,000 qubit UK-based system over 3-4 years
- Continued collaboration with Riverlane on error correction and error mitigation research

## Investor takeaways

- **199% revenue YoY growth** on small base ($1.47M → $4.4M) - meaningful inflection but still tiny in absolute terms
- **Cash position is the dominant tactical strength**: $569M liquid, no debt
- **Operating burn ~$16M/quarter** (~$65M annualized); cash runway >7 years before any further raise
- GAAP net income is **not operational** - $53.7M gain is from warrant liability fair value change (non-cash)
- Share count growth from 332.3M is modest (+1M) sequentially; major dilution already occurred in 2025
- The $100M UK investment is the company's investment - not capital received; structurally a use of cash
