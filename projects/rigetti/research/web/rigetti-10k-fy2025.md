# Rigetti Computing, Inc. (NASDAQ: RGTI) - Form 10-K FY2025

**Source:** Annual Report on Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, filed via SEC EDGAR (Accession 000110465926023454). Filed March 4, 2026. Auditor opinion dated March 4, 2026, Spokane, Washington.

## 1. Business Description

Rigetti is a vertically integrated builder of gate-model, superconducting quantum computers. Its mission is "to be at the forefront of superconducting quantum computing." Products:

- **Novera QPU** (commercially launched December 2023): a 9-qubit chip with tunable couplers plus a 5-qubit test chip, based on the Ankaa-class architecture. The first commercially sold QPU. Sold to Montana State University (December 2024) and in 2025 received purchase orders for two Novera systems totaling approximately $5.7 million.
- **Ankaa-3** (Q4 2024): 84-qubit single-chip system; achieved 99.0% median two-qubit gate fidelity. Available via Amazon Braket.
- **Cepheus-1-36Q** (Q2 2025 launch): 36-qubit flagship multi-chip system made of four 9-qubit chiplets; "the industry's largest multi-chip quantum computer." Median 99.6% two-qubit gate fidelity (internal testing, January 2026), 76 ns CZ gate.
- **Cepheus-1-108Q** (January 2026): 108-qubit system built from twelve 9-qubit chiplets; 99.0% median two-qubit gate fidelity.
- **Rigetti Quantum Cloud Services (QCS)**: proprietary cloud platform, operating since 2017. Also distributed via Amazon Braket and Microsoft Azure Quantum.
- **Rigetti Foundry Services**: in-house Fab-1 wafer fabrication facility delivers superconducting quantum chips to academia, defense labs and national labs.

**Customer types and ecosystem partners disclosed:** Enterprise (AWS, Standard Chartered Bank, Moody's, HSBC); U.S. government (DARPA, DOE, AFRL, NASA / NASA Quantum AI Lab, Fermilab/SQMS); international government (India's C-DAC, Innovate UK); algorithm/software partners (Phasecraft, Riverlane, Q-CTRL); cloud distribution (AWS, Microsoft Azure, Strangeworks); strategic supplier (Quanta Computer). Headcount: 164 globally (162 full-time) as of March 1, 2026. Operations in U.S., U.K., Australia (Adelaide), Canada and India (Mumbai).

**Geographic revenue mix (FY2025):** United States $3.7M (53%), Europe $3.2M (45%), Asia and others $0.2M (3%). Non-U.S. revenue was 47.3% of total.

**Patents:** 121 issued and 160 pending as of December 31, 2025.

## 2. Risk Factors (Item 1A) - Top Material Risks

1. **Limited operating history; missed milestones:** "We have in the past failed to meet publicly announced milestones and may fail to meet projected technological milestones in the future. We have in the past changed our technology roadmap, including the anticipated milestones and timing thereof, including in each of the years ended December 31, 2018, 2022, 2023 and 2025."

2. **History of operating losses:** "We incurred net losses of $216.2 million and $201.0 million for the years ended December 31, 2025, and 2024, respectively. As of December 31, 2025, we had an accumulated deficit of $771.0 million... we may never become profitable."

3. **Capital intensity / future financing risk:** "If we are unable to obtain sufficient capital we would be unable to fund our operations and may be required to evaluate alternatives, which could include dissolving and liquidating our assets... and/or seeking protection under bankruptcy laws."

4. **Technical / fidelity / quantum advantage risk:** "No current quantum computers, including our quantum hardware, have reached quantum advantage or LFTQC, and may never reach QA or LFTQC... Quantum computing technology, including QA and LFTQC, may take years or decades to be realized, if ever."

5. **Competition from much larger players:** "Major companies now developing both quantum hardware and software include IBM, Google, Microsoft, IonQ, D-Wave, Quantinuum and PsiQuantum, among others... Many of our competitors have substantially greater brand recognition, customer relationships, and financial, technical and other resources." Sovereign-funded competitors include China, Russia, Canada, Australia, the U.K. and the EU.

6. **Customer concentration:** "Revenue from U.S. government entities accounted for 48.0% and 54.2% of our total revenue for the years ended December 31, 2025 and 2024, respectively."

7. **Government-contract dependence:** "Sales to government entities comprised 90.2% and 89.4% of our total revenue [for 2025 and 2024]." Risks include termination for convenience, audits, debarment, False Claims Act exposure, and budget/sequestration risk.

8. **Cloud-platform dependency:** "Our customer agreement with AWS remains in effect until (i) terminated for convenience, which we may do for any reason... or (ii) terminated for cause." AWS and Azure simultaneously compete and distribute.

9. **Single-site supply chain (Fab-1):** "Any significant damage to our quantum research facilities or Fab-1 may disrupt our operations and could have a material adverse impact on our business." Sole source for proprietary multi-chip processors; Fremont site exposure to earthquakes explicitly cited.

10. **Export controls:** "Our products and technologies are subject to U.S. export control and import laws and regulations, including the U.S. Export Administration Regulations, U.S. Customs regulations, and various economic and trade sanctions regulations administered by the U.S. Treasury Department's Office of Foreign Assets Controls... Regulations related to quantum computing are currently evolving and we face risks associated with changes to these regulations." (No specific ITAR or BIS designation disclosed.)

11. **Bayh-Dole / government IP rights:** "Some of our intellectual property has been or may be conceived or developed through government-funded research" subject to "march-in" rights, U.S.-manufacture preferences, and license obligations to the U.S. government.

12. **Warrant liability / fair-value volatility:** "Our warrants...are accounted for as liabilities and the changes in value of our Public Warrants and Private Warrants could have a material effect on our financial results." The change in fair value of derivative warrant liabilities was a $(150.6)M charge to FY2025 P&L.

13. **Dilution risk:** "The exercise of our outstanding warrants and stock options and vesting of our outstanding restricted stock units increases the number of shares eligible for future resale in the public market, resulting in dilution to our stockholders."

14. **Public-company / SRC-loss costs:** Rigetti will lose Smaller Reporting Company scaled-disclosure status starting with Q1 2026 10-Q; emerging growth company status terminates no later than December 31, 2026, triggering full Section 404(b) auditor attestation.

15. **Key-person dependence:** "We are highly dependent on our ability to attract and retain senior executive leadership and other key employees, such as quantum physicists, software engineers and other key technical employees."

16. **Past material weaknesses:** "We have in the past identified material weaknesses in our internal control over financial reporting, leading to a restatement of our financial statements for prior periods." (Disclosed as remediated; see Section 8.)

17. **Stock-price volatility:** During FY2025 Common Stock ranged from a low of $5.95 (January 13, 2025) to a high of $58.15 (October 14, 2025).

**Export-control posture:** Subject to EAR, OFAC sanctions, and U.S. Customs regulations. No ITAR designation, no Bureau of Industry and Security ("BIS") specific reference, no Section 232/301 tariff disclosure. Regulatory language is forward-looking and general.

## 7. Competition (Item 1)

Named competitors:
- Large tech with broad quantum programs: **IBM, Google, Microsoft, Amazon**.
- Gate-model and other quantum-hardware competitors: **IonQ, D-Wave, Quantinuum, PsiQuantum**.
- Sovereign-funded research organizations: China, Russia, Canada, Australia, U.K. and the EU.

Rigetti's positioning: superconducting modality (claimed 1,000x faster gate speeds than trapped-ion/neutral-atom modalities); patented multi-chip "chiplet" architecture as a path to scale; full-stack vertical integration through Fab-1; open modular architecture allowing third-party integration; QCS cloud platform delivered both directly and via AWS Braket / Azure Quantum.
