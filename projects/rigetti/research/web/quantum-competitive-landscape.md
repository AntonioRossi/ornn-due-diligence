## Three dominant architectures

| Architecture | Companies | Pros | Cons |
|--------------|-----------|------|------|
| Superconducting | Google, IBM, **Rigetti** | Fast gates, lithography-scalable, mature manufacturing | Decoherence; cryogenic infrastructure |
| Ion-trap | IonQ, Quantinuum | High fidelity, all-to-all connectivity | Slow gate speed |
| Photonic / topological | Microsoft (Majorana), QUBT, PsiQuantum | Long-term scaling story | Early stage; less proven |

## Rigetti's specific positioning vs cohort

- **Differentiator vs ion-trap (IonQ/Quantinuum)**: 50-70ns gate speed (~1,000× faster) per Rigetti claims
- **Differentiator vs IBM/Google**: pure-play, not embedded in larger cloud business
- **Differentiator vs all peers**: in-house Fab-1 (industry's first dedicated quantum device manufacturing facility)
- **Disadvantage vs IonQ**: smaller revenue ($7M vs $130M); slower commercial momentum
- **Disadvantage vs IBM/Google**: incomparable balance sheets ($569M cash vs Alphabet's $130B+)
- **Disadvantage vs D-Wave**: D-Wave has near-term commercial revenue from annealing-suited optimization workloads

## NISQ era framing

- All current systems still in NISQ (Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum) era
- Leading systems: low hundreds to low thousands of physical qubits
- Error rates require aggressive correction
- Commercial "killer app" remains elusive
- **Google Willow** (Dec 2024) achieved "below-threshold" QEC — major industry milestone but no commercial application yet

## DARPA QBI 2033 target

- Determine if any quantum computing approach can achieve **utility-scale operation by 2033**
- 15 finalists selected; Rigetti is one
- Rigetti CEO Kulkarni: "3-4 years away from demonstrating quantum advantage" (Oct 2025) → ~2029-2030 target

## Investor takeaways

- **Quantum is a real research and engineering story but a thin commercial business** across the entire cohort
- Rigetti is **commercially smaller** than IonQ ($7M vs $130M revenue) but **technically distinctive** (chiplet + Fab-1)
- IBM and Google have **infinitely deeper pockets** and meaningful enterprise sales motions — pure-plays carry binary scientific bet risk
- D-Wave is the only pure-play with **real commercial workloads today** (optimization via annealing)
- For a public-equity allocator: pure-plays belong in a clearly-sized speculative sleeve; treat as decade-horizon idea
