## Investor takeaways

- **Revenue declined 34% YoY in 2025** (lumpy on-prem system shipments) despite Q4 2024 having been a strong comparison
- **Q1 2026 revenue inflection ($4.4M) validates the FY2026 narrative** - but full-year sustainability still in question
- **R&D spend up 23%** while revenue down — investment phase, not commercialization phase
- **Massive dilution in 2025**: 68% weighted-share increase YoY
- **Non-GAAP operating loss ~$50M/yr** is the most relevant burn proxy
- **$216M GAAP net loss is mostly non-cash** from warrant fair-value changes - structurally important to back out
- Accumulated deficit topped $770.9M at year end - the cumulative shareholder dilution cost of the technology bet to date
