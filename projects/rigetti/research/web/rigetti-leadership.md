## Dr. Subodh Kulkarni — President & CEO

- Joined Rigetti as President & CEO in **December 2022**
- **30+ years semiconductor industry experience**
- **Prior CEO of CyberOptics Corporation** (developer/manufacturer of high-precision sensors and inspection systems for the semiconductor and electronics industry)
  - CEO from 2014 until CyberOptics was **acquired by Nordson Corporation in November 2022** (the acquisition cleared at ~$54/share, ~$390M total transaction value)
- Prior CEO of **Prism Computational Sciences** (semiconductor software tools); currently Chairman of the Board
- Earlier: CTO and SVP at **Imation** (scalable storage and data security); R&D and management positions at **3M Corporation and IBM**
- Education: **B.S. Chemical Engineering, IIT Mumbai**; **M.S. and Ph.D. Chemical Engineering, MIT**
- Currently also on the Board of **KeyTronic Corporation** (publicly traded EMS company)

## Investor takeaways

- **Kulkarni is a credentialed semiconductor industry CEO** — not a quantum-research academic. His value proposition is operational discipline, semiconductor commercialization experience, and public-company execution.
- The CyberOptics → Nordson sale at ~$390M demonstrates Kulkarni has executed a successful public-company exit in a related semiconductor inspection space.
- Board appears thin on **quantum physics** depth (no obvious Nobel-class or famous quantum researcher) but appropriate for a commercialization-stage public company.
- **Founders (Chad Rigetti, original co-founders)** are not listed in current leadership — Chad Rigetti departed as CEO in December 2022 and Kulkarni was brought in. This represents a complete CEO handoff to professional management for the public-market era.
- Director Michael Clifton's Falfurrias Capital Partners background suggests private-equity board representation (Falfurrias led pre-SPAC investment).
