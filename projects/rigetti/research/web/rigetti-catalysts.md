# Rigetti Computing — Key Catalyst Events (chronological)

Compiled from rigetti.com news + investors.rigetti.com releases
Scraped: 2026-05-27

## 2025

### April 3, 2025 — DARPA Quantum Benchmarking Initiative (QBI) selection

- Rigetti selected as one of 15 finalists for DARPA QBI
- Primary goal: determine if any quantum computing approach can achieve utility-scale operation **by 2033**
- Stage A: 6-month performance period, worth **up to $1M** upon milestone completion
- Source: globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/04/03/3055591

### April 30, 2025 — Quanta Computer $35M strategic investment closed

- Quanta Computer (TWSE: 2382.TW), a Taiwanese contract-manufacturing giant, closed previously announced investment
- Purchased ~$35M of Rigetti common stock at approximately **$11.59 per share**
- Tied to strategic collaboration agreement
- Source: globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/04/30/3071288

### June 11, 2025 — $350M ATM equity offering completed

- Sales of $350M gross proceeds of common stock pursuant to previously disclosed ATM program
- Post-offering position: **~$575M cash, equivalents, and AFS investments with no debt**
- Source: globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/06/11/3097457

### Aug 12, 2025 — 36-qubit Cepheus-1-36Q general availability

- Industry's largest multi-chip quantum computer at the time
- Halves two-qubit gate error rate
- Based on four 9-qubit chiplets tiled together

### Sep 30, 2025 — Novera purchase orders for two quantum computing systems (~$5.7M)

### Oct 28, 2025 — NVIDIA NVQLink support

- Rigetti supports NVIDIA NVQLink — NVIDIA's open platform for AI supercomputer–quantum integration
- Source: globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/10/28/3175952

### Nov 10, 2025 — Q3 2025 results + 2026/2027 technology roadmap updates

## 2026

- GA pushed to end of Q1 2026 due to tunable coupler iteration
- Q4 2025 fidelity status: median 2-qubit fidelity 99% (108Q), 99.6% (36Q), 99.7% (9Q)
- Source: investors.rigetti.com/news-releases/.../rigetti-computing-provides-update-108-qubit-system

- Chiplet-based 108-qubit superconducting quantum computer

- **DARPA Quantum Benchmarking Initiative** (Stage A, up to $1M)

## Investor takeaways

- 2025–2026 has been an **intensely catalyst-rich period**: $350M ATM, Quanta investment, NVIDIA partnership, DARPA selection, C-DAC order, 108-qubit GA, $100M DoC LOI
- The DoC LOI is the largest single catalyst — but contemplates **equity dilution to the government**
- Government-revenue share is high: DoC, DARPA, AFRL, AFOSR, UK Innovate, NQCC, C-DAC India, Saskatchewan/University buyers, SQMS
- The Quanta Computer partnership is strategically meaningful: contract manufacturing relationship for hardware scaling
- DARPA QBI 2033 utility-scale target sets the official timeline frame: "3-4 years from quantum advantage" (per Kulkarni) maps to ~2029-2030
