# Management Diligence Request List — Rigetti Computing (NASDAQ: RGTI)

Date: 2026-05-27
Recipient: Investor Relations (IR@rigetti.com); Dr. Subodh Kulkarni (CEO); Jeffrey Bertelsen (CFO)
Audience for this document: phase-two diligence team

## Priority 0 — Required before any capital commitment

### Department of Commerce LOI

1. **DoC LOI definitive transaction agreement timeline**: expected signing window, contingencies, conditions precedent.
2. **Equity stake mechanics**: pricing formula, total shares issuable, lockup, voting rights, board observer rights, anti-dilution provisions, transfer restrictions.
3. **Use of proceeds disclosure**: project-by-project allocation of the $100M over three years.
4. **R&D milestone payment schedule** and clawback provisions.

### Financial reporting integrity

5. **Auditor going-concern language**: verbatim from FY2025 10-K and any going-concern statements in proxy or 10-Q filings.
6. **Internal Controls Over Financial Reporting**: most recent ICFR auditor opinion (unqualified vs material weakness); remediation timelines if applicable.
7. **Customer concentration**: top 10 customers as % of FY2025 and Q1 2026 revenue; geographic concentration disclosure.
8. **Deferred revenue / backlog**: opening and closing balances; signed but undelivered orders.

### Technology execution

9. **108Q fidelity trajectory**: month-by-month plan from current 99.1% median 2-qubit CZ to the committed 99.5% target; expected timeline for next chip iteration.
10. **Tunable coupler interactions**: full technical disclosure of issues identified during Q4 2025 testing; remediation plan; risk of further iterations at higher qubit counts.

## Priority 1 — Required before sizing decision

### Commercial trajectory

11. **Q2 2026 revenue guidance**: range, key contracts contributing, customer mix.
12. **Sustainability of Q1 2026 inflection**: was $4.4M one-time (e.g., Novera shipment lump) or run-rate sustainable?
13. **C-DAC India H2 2026 deployment**: acceptance criteria, milestone schedule, payment terms ($8.4M).
14. **UK $100M investment plan**: capex breakdown, employment commitments, joint partners (academic / commercial), timeline year-by-year.
15. **Standard Chartered commercial pilot**: stage, revenue contribution, expansion potential.

### Capital structure

16. **ATM utilization plans for FY2026**: program limits, recent issuance pace, conditions for additional issuance.
17. **Warrant liability composition**: outstanding warrant series, expiration, exercise prices, dilutive impact at $25 and $50 stock.
18. **2027 cash burn projection**: opex growth model with R&D, manufacturing scale-up, capex for Fab-1 expansion.
19. **Path to GAAP breakeven**: management's modeled revenue threshold and timing.

### Competitive positioning

20. **IBM Heron R2 quantum advantage 2026 target**: how Rigetti positions if IBM demonstrates first.
21. **IonQ FY26 commercial momentum**: how Rigetti differentiates if IonQ continues outpacing on revenue.
22. **D-Wave annealing commercial workloads**: any gate-based competitor wins on optimization use cases?
23. **NVIDIA NVQLink ecosystem**: which joint customers will be announced and when?

### Government revenue exposure

24. **DARPA QBI Stage B progression**: when does Rigetti expect Stage A milestones to complete; what does Stage B look like?
25. **AFRL / AFOSR contract pipeline**: active contracts, run-rate, follow-on opportunities.
26. **CHIPS Act dependence**: what % of FY26-FY28 R&D budget assumes government co-funding?

1. Walk us through the DoC LOI definitive transaction timeline and the equity-stake mechanics.
2. What is the auditor's going-concern conclusion in the FY2025 10-K? Any ICFR material weakness?
3. What is Q2 2026 revenue guidance, and is the Q1 $4.4M run-rate sustainable through year-end?
4. When does Cepheus-1-108Q reach the committed 99.5% median 2Q fidelity, and what is the next chip iteration timeline?
5. Top 10 customer concentration — what percentage of FY2025 revenue is the largest single customer?
6. How does Rigetti's chiplet architecture defend if Google announces a Willow successor before Toccata-equivalent milestones?
7. What is the path to GAAP operating breakeven, and at what revenue level?
8. Is the active ATM facility expected to be utilized in FY2026?
9. What is the projected R&D opex run-rate at the end of 2026 and 2027?
10. How does Rigetti respond if IBM's Heron R2 demonstrates a commercial quantum advantage application before Rigetti's 200+ qubit milestone?
