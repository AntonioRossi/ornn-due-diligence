# Investment Committee Memo — Rigetti Computing (NASDAQ: RGTI)

Date: 2026-05-27
Audience: Professional investors / institutional allocators
Recommendation: **Authorize speculative-sleeve initiation post-DoC LOI definitive agreement; size conservatively (≤1.5% of speculative book).** Rigetti is the only superconducting pure-play quantum company combining an in-house chiplet fab [2], a credentialed semiconductor-industry CEO with a successful public-company exit (CyberOptics → Nordson, $390M) [7], a generally-available 108-qubit system at 99.1% median 2-qubit fidelity [5], and a non-binding $100M U.S. Department of Commerce letter of intent with associated equity issuance to the federal government [6]. Risks are structural: revenue has declined four years running ($13.1M FY22 → $7.1M FY25) [11], the stock trades at ~189× forward 2027 consensus revenue [11][10], and dilution has been severe (weighted shares 184M → 332M, +80% in 18 months) [4]. Phase-two diligence must clear the DoC definitive transaction, going-concern language, customer concentration, and Cepheus-1 fidelity scaling.

## 1. Why this matters now

Rigetti is approaching three discrete catalysts within an eight-week window:

- **$100M DoC LOI to definitive agreement** — signed May 21, 2026. Award up to $100M over three years under CHIPS Research and Development Office Broad Agency Announcement; Department of Commerce to receive an equity stake "consistent with the total amount of the funding" [6]. At current $8.33B market cap, ~1.2% dilution.
- **108-qubit Cepheus-1-108Q general availability validation** — deployed April 7, 2026; live on Rigetti QCS, AWS Braket, Microsoft Azure Quantum, qBraid [3]. Multi-cloud distribution lowers customer acquisition friction.
- **Q2 2026 earnings (expected August 2026)** — will validate or refute the Q1 revenue inflection ($4.4M, +199% YoY) [3].

## 2. Differentiation vs the cohort

- **Vertical integration**: Rigetti owns chip design + Fab-1 fabrication + control electronics + cloud delivery — a profile matched only by IBM (Yorktown) and Google (Santa Barbara) among quantum companies [2][12]. Pure-play peers (IonQ, D-Wave, QUBT) do not run their own fabs at industry scale.
- **Chiplet modular architecture**: Cepheus-1 systems compose 9-qubit chiplets into larger machines (4 chiplets → 36Q, 12 chiplets → 108Q). Validated path to 1,000+ qubits if scaling continues [2][3].
- **Speed advantage**: 50-70ns gate times — Rigetti's claim is ~1,000× faster than trapped-ion modalities (IonQ, Quantinuum) [4][12].
- **Fab-1**: "industry's first dedicated and integrated quantum device manufacturing facility" — structural IP barrier [2].
- **IQT Research top-4 ranking**: alongside IBM, Quantinuum, and IonQ [12].
- **Sell-side breadth**: 12 firms covering (AGP, B. Riley, Benchmark, Cantor Fitzgerald, Cowen, Craig-Hallum, Jefferies, Mizuho, Needham, Northland, Rosenblatt, Wedbush) [9] — institutional liquidity.

## 5. Risk profile

### 5.1 Valuation (P0)

- **Market cap $8.33B at $24.75/share**; 52-week range $10.30 - $58.15 [10]
- **Price/Sales (ttm) ~805×**; **forward 2027 P/S ~189×** at consensus $44M revenue [11]
- **Motley Fool bear thesis** explicitly predicts stagnation or decline over next 12 months [11]
- **Stock declined 57% from $58 ATH** — sentiment cycle risk

### 5.2 Commercial (P0)

- Revenue declined every year FY2022 → FY2025: $13.1M → $12.0M → $10.8M → $7.1M [11]
- Q1 2026 inflection ($4.4M) needs to be **sustained**, not a single-quarter lump
- Revenue is "lumpy ... driven by an uneven mix of government and research contracts, occasional Novera shipments, and occasional use of QCS platform" [11]
- **Quantum advantage "3-4 years away" per Kulkarni** [11][12] — commercial product-market fit not in sight before 2029-2030

### 5.3 Dilution and capital markets (P0)

- **80% weighted-share increase in 18 months** (184M → 332M) [4]
- Active ATM program still in effect (continuous dilution capability)
- DoC LOI contemplates additional ~1.2% dilution via federal equity stake [6]
- Q1 2026 GAAP net income of $33.1M is **non-cash** (warrant liability fair-value change) [3]

### 5.4 Technology execution (P1)

- 108-qubit Cepheus-1-108Q reached GA late (Q1 2026 vs original 2025 target) [5]
- Median 2Q fidelity on 108Q is 99.1% (CZ) — below 99.5% target [3][5]
- "Tunable coupler interactions emerge at higher qubit counts" — flagged in Q4 2025 filings [4]
- Scaling to 1,000+ qubits over 3-4 years is **architectural bet** not proven path

### 5.5 Competition (P1)

- **IonQ** is ~18× larger by revenue ($130M vs $7.1M FY25) and guides $225-245M FY26 [12]
- **IBM Heron R2** targets quantum advantage demonstration in 2026 [12]
- **Google Willow** achieved "below-threshold" QEC in Dec 2024 — flagship modality competitor with infinite balance sheet [12]
- **D-Wave** has near-term commercial revenue (annealing-specific) that Rigetti lacks [12]

### 5.6 Government / political (P1)

- Trump administration CHIPS Act funding stance is supportive (Lutnick LOI language) [6]
- Reliance on government contracts means policy reversal is a risk
- DoC equity stake is unusual structure — sets precedent worth monitoring

## 6. Recommendation

**Authorize speculative-sleeve initiation post-DoC LOI definitive agreement.** Specifically:

1. **Phase-two diligence scope**:
   - DoC definitive transaction agreement and equity stake mechanics
   - Auditor going-concern language and ICFR opinion (10-K close review)
   - Top-10 customer concentration disclosure
   - 108Q fidelity trajectory to 99.5% target
   - Q2 2026 revenue sustainability of $4M+/quarter trajectory

2. **Sizing constraint**: ≤1.5% of speculative book. **Do NOT lead size**. Rigetti is suitable as part of a barbell quantum exposure (Alphabet + IBM as funded diversified exposure; Rigetti/IonQ as asymmetric pure-play call options).

3. **Entry points**:
   - Initial 50% of target weight: post-DoC definitive agreement signing
   - Remaining 50%: post-Q2 2026 earnings if revenue $4.5M+ confirms inflection
   - Hard exit triggers: (a) DoC definitive agreement fails to materialize within 90 days; (b) Q2 2026 revenue <$3M reverts to lumpy pattern; (c) any going-concern auditor language in upcoming filings; (d) IonQ or IBM announces commercial quantum advantage application first

4. **Allocation hedge**: pair RGTI long with IBM and Alphabet anchors in the same theme to capture beta while limiting binary scientific-bet risk.
