# Management Response Evaluation Guide — Rigetti Computing

Date: 2026-05-27
Audience: investment-committee members and phase-two diligence evaluators

This guide pairs each of the 10 first-call questions in `management-diligence-request-list.md` with what a credible answer looks like (green flag), what a partial answer looks like (yellow flag), and what disqualifies the response (red flag). At the end, a decision framework summarizes how individual responses aggregate to a go/no-go for speculative-sleeve initiation.

---

## Q1 — DoC LOI definitive transaction timeline + equity stake mechanics

- **Green**: Specific definitive agreement target date (e.g., "by Q3 2026"); equity stake priced at VWAP with 6-month lockup; no special voting rights; standard anti-dilution; documented project-by-project use of proceeds.
- **Yellow**: Definitive agreement "expected in coming quarters" without specific date; equity mechanics still under negotiation.
- **Red**: Major terms (price, lockup, voting rights, board observer) undisclosed even under NDA; or LOI structure includes preferential rights to the federal government that override normal shareholder protections.

## Q2 — Going-concern + ICFR auditor opinions

- **Green**: Clean (unqualified) auditor opinion; no going-concern language; no material ICFR weakness disclosed. Walks through the audit firm's specific opinion language.
- **Yellow**: Clean opinion but ICFR has remediation plan in progress (common in post-SPAC companies); committed timeline to resolve.
- **Red**: Going-concern language present in FY2025 10-K; or material weakness in ICFR without remediation timeline; or audit firm change without explanation.

## Q3 — Q2 2026 revenue guidance + sustainability of Q1

- **Green**: Specific Q2 range (e.g., "$4-6M") backed by committed backlog; explicit segmentation of Novera vs Cepheus vs QCS revenue; clarity that Q1 was driven by previously deferred deliveries that recur in Q2.
- **Yellow**: General "significant YoY growth in 2026" statement; no specific Q2 guidance.
- **Red**: Q1 revenue confirmed as one-time lumpy ($4.4M not run-rate); Q2 expected to revert to <$2M; or refusal to discuss revenue outlook.

## Q4 — 108Q fidelity to 99.5%

- **Green**: Specific monthly milestone roadmap to 99.5% with named technical interventions (e.g., next chip iteration June 2026, fidelity testing July 2026); willingness to share TN10-equivalent benchmark data.
- **Yellow**: "Targeting H2 2026 for 99.5%" with high-level commitment but no monthly milestones.
- **Red**: Cannot commit to 99.5% within FY2026; or current 99.1% is the realistic plateau; or refuses to address tunable-coupler complexity from Q4 2025.

## Q5 — Customer concentration

- **Green**: Top 10 customer concentration disclosed (e.g., "top customer = 18% of FY2025 revenue; top 5 = 65%"); customer types named (government labs, universities, commercial); revenue split by region.
- **Yellow**: Aggregate concentration disclosed but customer names protected by NDA.
- **Red**: Top customer > 50% of revenue; or concentration data simply not tracked at C-suite level.

## Q6 — Defense vs Google Willow successor

- **Green**: Clear differentiation pitch: chiplet modular architecture as a manufacturing IP advantage; in-house Fab-1 as throughput moat; explicit recognition that Google has scale advantage but Rigetti has commercialization speed; named upcoming Rigetti milestone (e.g., 200+ qubit chiplet system) on a defined date.
- **Yellow**: Generic "we have a different approach" without timeline-specific competitive milestones.
- **Red**: Defensiveness; inability to articulate a competitive moat that survives a Google or IBM announcement.

## Q7 — Path to GAAP breakeven

- **Green**: Specific revenue threshold (e.g., "GAAP breakeven at $125M annual revenue with current opex base"); timeline ("3-4 years out at current trajectory"); levers to accelerate (margin expansion via on-prem sales mix, R&D leverage as systems scale).
- **Yellow**: Breakeven "in 4-5 years" without specific revenue threshold or operating leverage modeling.
- **Red**: Cannot model breakeven path; or breakeven requires market-cap-justifying revenue (e.g., $200M+) the consensus does not project until 2028+.

## Q8 — ATM utilization in FY2026

- **Green**: ATM facility size disclosed; commitment to no further issuance in FY26 absent specific opportunity (e.g., acquisition); historical pattern (Q1 2026 saw zero issuance) reinforced.
- **Yellow**: ATM remains active but "no current plans for utilization"; reserved as optionality.
- **Red**: ATM expected to be utilized; or refuses to commit to a non-issuance window even for one quarter.

## Q9 — R&D opex run-rate

- **Green**: Specific FY26 and FY27 R&D opex ranges (e.g., "FY26 R&D $80-90M; FY27 $95-110M"); SG&A flat or modestly growing; capex plans for Fab-1 expansion disclosed.
- **Yellow**: Directional commentary ("R&D will continue growing in line with revenue"); no numeric range.
- **Red**: R&D opex expansion uncapped; SG&A growing faster than revenue.

## Q10 — Response to IBM Heron R2 quantum advantage announcement

- **Green**: Acknowledges IBM lead; explains differentiation (Rigetti's chiplet manufacturing IP vs IBM's monolithic; Rigetti's pure-play scale vs IBM's diversified portfolio); pivot strategy outlined (e.g., become the chiplet manufacturing supplier; license IP).
- **Yellow**: Diplomatic non-answer ("we follow our own roadmap").
- **Red**: Dismisses competitive threat without substance; or no contingency strategy.

## Decision framework

- **9 or 10 greens, 0 reds**: Proceed to full phase-two diligence (audit close-out review, customer reference calls, post-DoC LOI signing). Initiate 50% of speculative-sleeve target post-DoC definitive agreement; complete the remaining 50% post-Q2 2026 earnings.
- **6-8 greens, ≤ 1 red**: Conditional proceed. Issue follow-up list with 30-day deadlines on the reds before any commitment. Initial position size capped at 30% of speculative-sleeve target.
- **3-5 greens, or 2 reds**: Pause. Either commercial readiness is overstated or our diligence questions need sharpening. Re-evaluate after Q2 2026 earnings.
- **2 reds in (Q2, Q3, Q5, Q7, Q8)**: Decline. These map to financial integrity + commercial trajectory + capital structure — the three pillars of the public-equity thesis. Any two fails together undermine the recommendation.

## Sizing rationale

If diligence clears:

- **Initial position**: 50% of speculative-sleeve target post-DoC definitive agreement (expected Q3 2026)
- **Layer 2**: Remaining 50% post-Q2 2026 earnings if revenue $4.5M+ confirms inflection
- **Maximum exposure**: 1.5% of speculative book (3% with barbell IBM+Alphabet hedging)
- **Hard exit triggers**:
  - DoC definitive agreement fails to materialize within 90 days of LOI
  - Q2 2026 revenue <$3M (reverts to lumpy pattern)
  - Any going-concern auditor language in upcoming filings
  - IonQ or IBM announces commercial quantum advantage application first
  - Stock breaks below $15 on no fundamental catalyst (signal of broader thematic deflation)
  - Material new ATM issuance (>$200M) within 6 months
